REIN HUBS

RESILIENCE, ENTREPRENEURSHIP, INNOVATION & ENVIRONMENT

REIN Hubs

For funders and partners

A Global Platform for Resilient, Inclusive, Nature-Based Innovation. Led by PVBLIC Foundation and CleantechHUB, backed by global family offices.

REIN currently operates five fully active hubs in Colombia, Perú, Costa Rica, Guatemala, and El Salvador, turning environmental challenges into investment-ready business opportunities for entrepreneurs across the bio-economy, renewable energy, circular economy, AI, data, and agriculture.

https://www.pvblic.org/rein-hubs — checked 21 Oct 2026

Three foundations

1

Country & Regional Hubs

The local and regional infrastructure where ideas are developed, ventures are built, and high-potential solutions are accelerated.

2

National Alignment

Integration with national development and resilience strategies to ensure ventures respond to country priorities and have pathways for adoption and scale.

3

Capital Infrastructure

Blended finance pathways connecting investment-ready ventures with multilateral, public, and private capital partners.

Pipeline funnel

Built on these three foundations, REIN moves innovators through a structured pathway from early-stage ideas to scalable, investment-ready ventures.
