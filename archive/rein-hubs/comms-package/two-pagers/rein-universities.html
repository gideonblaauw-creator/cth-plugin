Why and how to host a REIN Hub

Anchored in national development strategies and hosted by academic institutions, REIN Hubs serve as localized engines for sustainable growth, resilience, and prosperity.

Model: public–private partnership with local university / corporate hosts.

Launch post, 23 May 2025: https://www.pvblic.org/rein-hubs-officially-launch-at-nature-summit-2025-a-new-era-of-environmental-innovation. Partnership line: decks/rein-academic-city-ghana/sources/academic-city-ghana-2026-04-23.html#overview

What each hub receives

A

Program Design & Delivery

Complete curriculum across the 4-stage pipeline. Bootcamps, mentorship frameworks, pitch coaching, workbooks, and evaluation rubrics — all provided.

B

Brand & Communications

Full REIN brand kit — logos, templates, social-media assets, presentation decks. Professional visual identity including a dedicated country logo option.

C

Technology Infrastructure

Kintec Academy platform for online learning, evidence tracking and certification. BookStack wiki for knowledge management.

D

Mentor Network

Curated mentor pool with mentor–mentee matching. Weekly mentorship cadence. International and local mentor access through the REIN network.

E

Global Competition Pathway

Direct entry to ClimateLaunchpad — Nationals → Regional → Global Final in Singapore, October 2026.

F

Visibility & Events

REIN Live webinar series. Presence at COP, Climate Week NYC, UNGA, CEEA Accra. Annual impact reporting and communications support.
