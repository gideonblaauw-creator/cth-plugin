## Program page — wording

Opening:

> A Global Platform for Resilient, Inclusive, Nature-Based Innovation
>
> Led by PVBLIC Foundation and CleantechHUB, backed by global family offices.
>
> Explore our 5 Hubs across Latin America
>
> What is REIN
>
> Resilience, Entrepreneurship, Innovation & Nature - REIN.
>
> What began as a U.S. Department of State pilot to expand the pipeline of clean technology and green startups in a few target locations has evolved into REIN, a permanent, multilateral platform managed by PVBLIC Foundation in partnership with CleantechHUB, expanding the model into new countries and regions.
>
> REIN currently operates five fully active hubs in Colombia, Perú, Costa Rica, Guatemala, and El Salvador, turning environmental challenges into investment-ready business opportunities for entrepreneurs across the bio-economy, renewable energy, circular economy, AI, data, and agriculture.

Three foundations, in the page’s words:

- **Country & Regional Hubs.** “The local and regional infrastructure where ideas are developed, ventures are built, and high-potential solutions are accelerated.”
- **National Alignment.** “Integration with national development and resilience strategies to ensure ventures respond to country priorities and have pathways for adoption and scale.”
- **Capital Infrastructure.** “Blended finance pathways connecting investment-ready ventures with multilateral, public, and private capital partners.”

Pathway names on the page, each with the sentence that follows it. The numbers 1, 2, 3, and 4 are on the page. This check does not assign a number to a name, because the extracted order is not enough to lock that pairing.

- **Ideation.** “Early-stage idea development for innovators and students exploring environmental solutions.”
- **Incubation.** “National incubation programs within each university hub, building viable ventures.”
- **Acceleration.** “Regional acceleration for high-potential ventures ready to scale across markets.”
- **Investment Readiness.** “Mentorship, ESG/MRV support, and investor engagement to secure growth capital.”

No week or month durations are on this page.
