Program

A Global Platform for Resilient, Inclusive, Nature-Based Innovation

Led by PVBLIC Foundation and CleantechHUB, backed by global family offices.

What began as a U.S. Department of State pilot to expand the pipeline of clean technology and green startups in a few target locations has evolved into REIN, a permanent, multilateral platform managed by PVBLIC Foundation in partnership with CleantechHUB, expanding the model into new countries and regions.

The model

Country & Regional Hubs

The local and regional infrastructure where ideas are developed, ventures are built, and high-potential solutions are accelerated.

National Alignment

Integration with national development and resilience strategies to ensure ventures respond to country priorities and have pathways for adoption and scale.

Capital Infrastructure

Blended finance pathways connecting investment-ready ventures with multilateral, public, and private capital partners.
