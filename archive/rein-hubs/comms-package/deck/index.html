What is REIN Hubs?

REIN Hubs (Resilience · Entrepreneurship · Innovation · Nature) is a pioneering public–private partnership that builds climate and supply-chain ventures — pairing local execution with regional leverage.

Through activation, incubation, acceleration, and growth programs, REIN Hubs backs early-stage climate and cleantech founders with capital, mentorship, and corporate access — operating physical hubs where entrepreneurs, researchers, and industry converge to build environment-positive, trade-ready companies.

Slide 8 of 9 · Support system

A multi-layered support system

REIN Hubs connects local founders to a global ecosystem — from a neighbourhood workshop to a corporate offtake contract.

At the Core — Founders

Early-stage climate, cleantech, circular-economy and resilient-supply ventures — with a deliberate focus on women and under-represented founders in emerging markets.

Direct Support

Mentor matching, bootcamps, workshops, pitch coaching and workbooks delivered through the Kintec Academy digital platform.

Network Access

University partners, European corporates, multilateral agencies, sponsor networks, FOSD family office network, UN SDG Joint Fund.

ClimateLaunchpad

Direct entry to the world's largest green business idea competition, with finals in Singapore each October — a continental scale-up route.

REIN Live & Global Events

REIN Live webinar series spotlighting hub achievements, with presence at COP, Climate Week NYC, UNGA, and the CEEA Accra summit.

Non-US Investor Network

European impact investors, EU corporate offtake, philanthropic grants, SAFE investments — intentional diversification away from US federal anchor funding.
