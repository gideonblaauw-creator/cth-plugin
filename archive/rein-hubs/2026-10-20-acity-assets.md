# Academic City assets for a REIN Ghana Hub

**Date pulled:** 20 October 2026  
**Status:** Internal draft for Gideon’s prep. Not for outbound. REIN HOLD applies.  
**Campus:** Academic City University, Property # 279-302, Agbogba, Haatso, Accra, Ghana.  
**Audience:** Prep for Laurence (PVBLIC) and the Pav / Academic City (ACity) conversation on a proposed REIN Ghana Hub.

Granola has no note that names ACity, Pav, Laurence, or a Ghana hub. The closest REIN pattern on file is a university entrepreneurship centre that already hosts climate programming. ACity’s Technology and Entrepreneurship Centre (TEC) is that kind of centre.

## How the assets contribute

ACity can host a hub inside spaces it already runs. TEC, the Connecting Climate Entrepreneurs (CCE) Lab, and the makerspace cover idea-to-prototype work. Engineering labs and the BioInnovation studios add hardware. Hostels, the health centre, and canteens cover a residential cohort. Media Lab and ACity Radio cover comms.

Public pages do not state room capacities. Nothing published confirms seats for 50 people, benches for 25 teams, or a desk for 4 growth teams. Durations that do match a REIN stage are called out below. All other sizes are unverified.

REIN stages used here:

| Stage | Published REIN shape |
|---|---|
| Ideation | 50 participants, 4 weeks |
| Incubation | 25 teams, 10 weeks |
| Acceleration | 10 teams, 12 weeks |
| Growth services | 4 teams, investor connections |
| Hub ops/comms | Campus operations and communications |

## Fit against the REIN numbers

- **Ideation, 50 people, 4 weeks.** Amphitheatres and classrooms exist. Seat counts do not. A freshman innovation course and CCE workshops are the programme analogues. A 4-week REIN syllabus is not on the site.
- **Incubation, 25 teams, 10 weeks.** TEC, CCE, MakeLab, and the engineering workshop are the physical core. The only team figure on TEC’s about page is a challenge designed for **10 student projects**. A 10-week clock is not stated.
- **Acceleration, 10 teams, 12 weeks.** Telesto is the cleanest match on **duration (12 weeks)** and on climate-tech startups. Its published headcount is a **13-startup session**, not a cap of 10, and the write-up is from 2023.
- **Growth, 4 teams.** TEC’s investor showcase language is real. A 4-team growth service is not described.
- **Ops.** Housing, 24/7 clinic, dining, and security can hold a residential cohort. Total beds are unpublished.
