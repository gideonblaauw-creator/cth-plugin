REIN × EcoForest: six ways to collaborate

Room. Stephen Keppel (PVBLIC) set this call to discuss REIN in Mexico. With him: Concha (PVBLIC), Jennifer Gonzalez (CleantechHUB), Gideon, and Fatima Montiel. Stephen has not said if he sees her as hub host, funder, or community partner.

Checked. ecoforestco2.com, 19 Oct 2026. Fatima Montiel is CEO of EcoForest Group / EcoForest CO₂, with offices in Durango and Mexico City. The site says she was the first woman to chair the National Commission of Young Entrepreneurs at COPARMEX (Confederación Patronal de la República Mexicana). It also says she co-founded DUA NetZero Consulting. A public LinkedIn profile matches both, and dates that presidency to March 2018–March 2021. This pass did not confirm the chair role on a COPARMEX page.

EcoForest’s own claims. Forest, grassland, and renewable credits. 538,216 hectares in Durango, Oaxaca, Yucatán, Chihuahua, and other states. 1,749,302 tCO₂e in available inventory. 215,000+ credits commercialized in the last 12 months. 70% of originated-project revenue to communities and land stewards. Treat every figure as their claim.

REIN frame. REIN (Resilience, Entrepreneurship, Innovation & Nature) is a joint program of the PVBLIC Foundation and CleantechHUB. University hubs run in Colombia, Peru, Guatemala, Costa Rica, and El Salvador. There is no Mexico hub. Each hub runs four stages a year. Ideation is 4 weeks and 50 people. Incubation is 10 weeks and 25 teams. Acceleration is 12 weeks and 10 teams. Growth is 12 weeks and 4 teams. Investor connections are the core of Growth. Universities need 2027 clarity by 1 Dec 2026. The FOSD family-office network is a separate route. Datalab and Nexus are the data platforms already in the program. PVBLIC can place teams in the United Nations General Assembly (UNGA) Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) Media Zone and at Climate Week NYC.

1.Mexico hub host

What. EcoForest anchors the first Mexico hub for 2027, in Durango or Mexico City, beside a university.

EcoForest gets. A named place in the five-country program, plus PVBLIC visibility in New York.

REIN gets. A Mexico path before universities lock 2027 plans on 1 Dec 2026.

Ask today. Will she name one city and one university she would anchor?

Risk. Hubs are university-hosted. A company anchor can clash with that model and with the December decision.

2.Cohort sponsor

What. EcoForest or DUA sponsors one stage of a hub that already exists, and does not run the hub.

EcoForest gets. Its name on one cohort and a first look at teams, inside a bounded role.

REIN gets. Support for Ideation, Incubation, Acceleration, or Growth, with no new country.

Ask today. Which single stage, and in which country that already has a hub?

Risk. A funding ask on a first call can stall the talk if she came to shape Mexico, not to pay for it.

3.Offtake channel

What. EcoForest’s buyer desk, including spot, forward, and offtake, becomes a first customer for REIN teams.

EcoForest gets. Vetted extra supply, or field-service startups inside projects it already sells.

REIN gets. A real buyer in Growth, beside the investor introductions that stage is built on.

Ask today. Will she open one door for teams from the five existing hubs: credits, or field services?

Risk. There is no Mexico cohort yet. She may also treat startup carbon developers as rivals, not as supply.

4.Community implementer

What. EcoForest runs a four-week Ideation inside one ejido or forest community it already works with.

EcoForest gets. Local ventures from families in its projects, without becoming the hub operator.

REIN gets. A Mexico test of Ideation, with no decision to open a hub.

Ask today. Will she name one site, such as San Bernardino de Milpillas Chico or Villa Ahumada, for 50 participants?

Risk. Community trust sits with EcoForest. A startup call can look extractive next to their revenue-share claim. Say who owns the ventures, and get community consent, before any date.

5.Data and MRV partner

What. One REIN digital measurement, reporting, and verification (dMRV) team pilots on part of EcoForest’s project area and shares reads with Datalab.

EcoForest gets. An outside check it can show buyers, while it keeps the registry and the sale.

REIN gets. A large field site for one startup, and a live use of Datalab and Nexus.

Ask today. Will she pick one project area for a short read-only pilot?

Risk. The site already claims field plots, ongoing measurement, satellite data, and blockchain credits. A pilot can undercut that story, or stall on who owns the data.

6.COPARMEX convening

What. Fatima brings a small group of Mexican companies as future buyers, mentors, and sponsors. This is not a hub.

EcoForest gets. A private-sector table about startups, not only about credit sales.

REIN gets. Named companies in Mexico while the university host is still undecided.

Ask today. Will she convene one company meeting before 1 Dec 2026?

Risk. The national young-entrepreneurs chair reads as a past role. She may have no current mandate to convene COPARMEX. Do not assume she does.

Top pick

Option 4, community implementer. It uses the asset the site actually shows: ties to ejidos and forest communities. It does not turn a company into a university. It does not open with a funding ask. It leaves the 1 Dec university decision untouched. If she offers to host or to fund, move to option 1 or 2. Do not give her that role before she speaks.

Opening question

“Fatima, before 1 December, which one role fits EcoForest: open a community, introduce a university host, or sponsor a cohort?”
