# EcoForest — how they make money

Internal note for Gideon Blaauw. 19 Oct 2026, before 13:00 COT. Do not send. **REPORTED** = company. **REGISTRY** = registry or a registry mirror. **ESTIMATE** = market price, not theirs.

**How they get paid.** EcoForest originates nature credits on Mexican community land and sells them, and it sources other people’s credits for buyers. The verified book is Climate Action Reserve improved forest management in Durango. The site also offers spot, forward, and offtake sales, plus I-RECs and CELs. DUA NetZero Consulting sells corporate climate advice. The ejido or community keeps the land and is the project owner. EcoForest’s keep is the residual after a claimed community share. No fee card, credit price, or investor was found.

## 1. Revenue lines

Own forest, grassland, and renewable credits are all on the site. Only the forest line is on a registry we opened. Spot, forward, offtake, and direct sales are **REPORTED**, as is custom sourcing by type, geography, and standard. That second book is brokerage. A commercial post calls it a desk of 16 projects. I-RECs and CELs are offered with no certificate IDs. They design, inventory, and register projects, but no day-rate is published. [ecoforestco2.com/en](https://ecoforestco2.com/en/) · [trading-desk post](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/eugenio-jimenez-dominguez_ifm-la-florida-activity-7386188913134067712-mWo-)

DUA NetZero is the consulting line. Fatima Montiel co-founded it. Rossana Reynoso has been named CEO. It sells footprints, targets, and compliance advice, and it describes automated carbon accounting. LinkedIn: founded 2026, about 3 people. No priced software product, so this is not a proven SaaS line. [duanetzero.com/en/home](https://duanetzero.com/en/home/)

## 6. Risks

These are removal credits for forest growth. The leakage rule was rewritten before the CCP label. A 2023 Rainforest Foundation UK review treats this protocol as a REDD+-type scheme with the usual baseline risk. It does not review these three projects. [RFUK](https://www.rainforestfoundationuk.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Carbon-Credits_final_ENG.pdf) Verified supply is three Durango forest projects. Carbon2Nature is both co-developer and the path to Iberdrola’s customers. Named end buyers are two unverified logos. Global improved-forest-management prices slipped in 2024, and Latin American REDD+ trades near a few dollars. Buyers can anchor there.

## 7. Where a startup program fits

- Ejidos already own the projects. A program fits at community ideation, before listing.
- MRV is field plots, plus a grassland satellite claim with no registry behind it. A data startup fits that step. Datalab is the data context. They publish no software product.
- The desk already buys and sells other people’s credits. Startups fit as suppliers or small offtakers. That is a channel, not a signed deal.
- Nobody public funds the years before issuance, and the community share leaves a thin residual. An investor link fits that gap. Fatima chaired COPARMEX’s young-entrepreneurs commission. That is a mentor door, not an offtake.

**Ask on the call.** Which contract is 70% and which is 79%. Who pays verification. Whether the 215,000 are CAR1941 retirements or brokered tonnes. What CS-0007 is. Whether any Siemens or BBVA tonne was retired. Which vintages carry the CCP label.
