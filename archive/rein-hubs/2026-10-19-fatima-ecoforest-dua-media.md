## 2. The company, in one page

EcoForest CO₂ says it develops and sells high-integrity carbon credits and connects forest communities with buyers. It says it is part of EcoForest Group and works with DUA Net Zero Consulting so a client can go from a field visit to a corporate climate plan. Offices: Durango City and Mexico City. Contact: contacto@ecoforestco2.com. Source: [ecoforestco2.com/en](https://ecoforestco2.com/en/).

Figures on that site (company claims, not a registry extract):

- 538,216 hectares in Durango, Oaxaca, Yucatán, Chihuahua, “and more states.”
- 215,000+ credits sold in the last 12 months.
- 1,749,302 tCO₂e in available inventory.
- 1,400+ families.
- **70%** of revenue from originated projects “goes directly to communities and land stewards.”

Named projects on the same page:

- **CAR1941** San Bernardino de Milpillas Chico, Durango. Improved forest management (IFM). About 153,000 ha. 260,000 Climate Reserve Tonnes (CRT). Core Carbon Principles (CCP) and Forest Stewardship Council (FSC) tags on the site.
- **CS-0007** Villa Ahumada, Chihuahua. Improved agricultural land management (ALM) / regenerative ranching. 7,000 ha. 260,000 CRT. Tags: “NASA satellite” and “blockchain credits.”
- **CS-0003** Hydropower Arcoiris, Jalisco. 8.4 MW. Same satellite and blockchain tags.

An EcoForest marketing PDF hosted by Carbonplace, dated **October 2025**, names EcoForestCO2 as developer of two removal projects: **CAR1847** Ejido La Ciudad (13,794 ha, 11,235 actively managed, 243 landowners, FSC) and **CAR1941** San Bernardino (153,202 ha, 145,346 actively managed, 100-year IFM, Tepehuano / Au’dam community, 1,148 communal owners, about 6,000 inhabitants). Source: [carbonplace.com PDF](https://carbonplace.com/files/8027d2db6871b5cbf41e8bfb25231b50ed489423.pdf).

**CAR1851 is UNVERIFIED.** That PDF does not mention La Florida or CAR1851. No public page reviewed here ties the ID CAR1851 to EcoForest.

**La Florida (Durango) is a different, confirmed project, without that ID.** Carbon2Nature (Iberdrola) says it is developing an IFM project with Ejido La Florida and Ecoforest in the Sierra Madre of Durango: 11,294 ha, 30 years, status Registered, organization Climate Action Reserve, capture target 1,721,555 (unit not labeled on the page), 36 IUCN Red List species. Community aims: decent jobs, technical training, new value chains. Restoration includes rehabilitation and reforestation inside the IFM area. Source: [carbon2nature.earth](https://carbon2nature.earth/en/proyecto/la-florida/).

A public LinkedIn post by Eugenio Jiménez (EcoForest commercial director), resharing Carbon2Nature, says the La Florida IFM covers 11,293 ha under the Mexico Forest Protocol, that the first international credits from Mexico with the CCP label were issued, and that the project is ICROA-endorsed and “CORSIA-eligible subject to national legislation.” EcoForest’s lines on that post say **at least 79%** of the revenue goes to the communities. Source: [linkedin.com/posts/eugenio-jimenez-dominguez](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/eugenio-jimenez-dominguez_ifm-la-florida-activity-7386188913134067712-mWo-). CRBN.credit lists “Ejido La Florida San Dimas, Durango” as a CAR forest project with about 130,000 tonnes issued, but the listing snippet does not show the project ID: [crbn.credit](https://crbn.credit/methodologies/car-forest-mx?page=2).

**Do not mix this with CAR1375.** BP’s voluntary-carbon disclosure lists **CAR1375 Carbono Otomí La Florida** as a different Mexico Forest Protocol project (start date 3 August 2018). BioForestal describes that project in **Hidalgo**, not Durango. Sources: [bp.com](https://www.bp.com/en/global/bp-supply-trading-and-shipping/documents-and-downloads/regulatory-resource-centre/voluntary-carbon-market-disclosures.html); [bioforestal.mx](https://bioforestal.mx/carbono-otomi-la-florida/).

## 4. Vision signals

**Communities.** The operating claim is a large community share: 70% of originated-project revenue on the company site, and at least 79% on the Carbon2Nature / La Florida post. They also say buyers get a report on how the money was used. Villa Ahumada, on the company site: 50+ families “out of poverty,” 200+ children with access to education, 7 Sustainable Development Goals, 71,000 CRT issued. Cloverly’s project aims for San Bernardino include equitable benefit distribution and better livelihoods; for La Ciudad, restoration, fire and sanitation brigades, and awareness of the forest as heritage. Carbon2Nature adds jobs, training, and new forest value chains at La Florida. No separate foundation or named social-program brand was found.

**Geography.** Public project and state names: Durango, Chihuahua, Oaxaca, Yucatán, Jalisco, plus “and more states.” Communities named on the site: Tepehuanos, San Miguel, Gavilanes, “+ to be confirmed.” **No public page found names another Latin American country as an EcoForest expansion target.** “Nuestra región” in the NACW post is not a country list. A LatAm rollout is **UNVERIFIED**.

**Products.** What they actually sell, on [ecoforestco2.com/en](https://ecoforestco2.com/en/): IFM forest credits, ALM grassland credits, renewable-energy attributes (International Renewable Energy Certificates / Certificados de Energía Limpia), and corporate decarbonization plans through DUA. Commercial forms: spot, forward, and offtake. Co-benefits (biodiversity, watersheds, social results) are described as part of the carbon credit, **not** as a separate biodiversity-credit product. Reforestation shows up as an activity inside IFM projects, **not** as a named afforestation, reforestation, and revegetation (ARR) product line. “NASA satellite” and “blockchain credits” are tags on Villa Ahumada and the Jalisco hydro project. Field MRV is described as permanent plots and continuous measurement plus third-party verification. A product branded dMRV was **not** found. (CAR itself has a Digital MRV module listed as in development; that is the registry’s roadmap, not an EcoForest product. Source: [climateactionreserve.org/how/protocols2](https://climateactionreserve.org/how/protocols2/).)

**Money and buyers.** No public funding round, investor, or raise was found. **UNVERIFIED.** Named buyers on the EcoForest site only: “Companies like Siemens, BBVA, and more already buy through us,” plus forwards. That sentence was not confirmed in a buyer disclosure. Carbon2Nature / Iberdrola is a project partner on La Florida, which is stronger than a logo claim. Cloverly is a marketplace listing, not proof of a sale.

**How she talks about the future.** Avoid first, then real natural assets, then traceable data. Nature as something a company owns and accounts for, not a donation. Carbon markets as a financial tool. Community share as the moral and commercial claim. International instinct, from the COPARMEX years, is supply chains and foreign capital, not a published country plan.

## 5. What DUA says about where she wants to grow

**Sites:** [duanetzero.com](https://duanetzero.com/) (canonical) and a second public page with the same offer, [duanetzero-co.lovable.app](https://duanetzero-co.lovable.app/). LinkedIn: [linkedin.com/company/dua-netzero-consulting](https://linkedin.com/company/dua-netzero-consulting) (public index: 3 employees, founded 2026).

**What it is.** DUA calls itself the consulting and strategic-intelligence arm of Grupo EcoforestCO2. The name is explained as Tepehuan for “the one who heals,” tied to the Sierra de Durango. Pitch: “the first Mexican climate-solvency infrastructure.” It says it shields profit from climate transition by turning compliance into a financial control panel, and by backing the balance sheet with EcoForest field projects.

**Services, from the website:**

- Mexican regulatory reporting: GRI, ISO 14001, NOM-001-SEMARNAT, NOM-035-STPS, CINIF sustainability notes, CNBV Single Issuer Circular, IFRS S1 and S2.
- Decarbonization roadmaps, GHG Protocol, cost per tonne, Scope 3, and the EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM).
- “World-class” software, an integrated management system, AI, and digital twins. No vendor is named.
- Climate-scenario work aimed at the cost of capital, the Finance Ministry green taxonomy, and SARAS for banks and lenders.
- Field biological assets through EcoForest, sold as real offsets rather than “theoretical speculation.”
- Process redesign toward circular models.

The Lovable page groups this as regulatory shield (CNBV, IFRS, SARAS, SBTi, CBAM), technical governance (ESG due diligence, footprint, ISO 14064, Scope 3, double materiality), and data (satellite measurement, automated reports, energy efficiency), then “close with Ecoforest assets.”

**Sectors on duanetzero.com:** manufacturing and industry (exporters, CBAM); financial services; logistics; water; energy (PPAs, clean-energy certificates); agribusiness (regenerative agriculture, verified credits, satellite traceability). The March 2026 wire, in Rossana Reynoso’s words, points the platform at cement, mining, and manufacturing, plus extended producer responsibility.

**Clients, case studies, countries.** No named DUA client and no case study were found. Geography in the copy is Mexico, plus readiness for European supply-chain rules. The Cámara Verde partnership is a Latin American chamber, not a project outside Mexico. **No public DUA page names a country expansion list.**

**Team the sites actually show.** Fátima Montiel (chair and group CEO) and Roxana Rosas (biological assets). The wire adds Rossana Reynoso as CEO. EcoForest’s own team page is larger than DUA: Rafael Alonso-Arenas (managing director), Roxana Rosas, Eugenio Jiménez (commercial; he claims a 16-project desk), Raúl Espinoza Bretado, Carlos Flores Coria, Ana Victoria Valdez, and Enrique Almeraz Reyes. Source: [ecoforestco2.com/en](https://ecoforestco2.com/en/). LinkedIn’s “7 employees” versus a “51–200” size band on the EcoForest company index conflict; use the named people, not the band.

**What this suggests she wants.** DUA is the growth story: Mexican exporters and lenders who must report climate risk, with EcoForest credits as the last step after avoidance. The Durango forests are the proof, not the whole company. The public ambition is corporate Mexico and global supply chains. It is not, on the pages reviewed, a plan to originate projects across Latin America.

## 6. Implications for a REIN Hubs conversation

- She may be offering two partnerships at once. EcoForest is a project developer and credit desk with communities in hand. DUA is a three-person, 2026 compliance firm with a launch press release and no public client. Ask which one the conversation is about before talking about startups.
- The public growth map is other Mexican states and Mexican industry (nearshoring, cement, mining, manufacturing, banks), not a named move into the rest of Latin America. Do not assume a regional platform.
- Community share is the line she can take into a partnership: 70% on the website, at least 79% on the La Florida post, plus education and poverty claims at Villa Ahumada. Those numbers do not yet match each other. Treat them as claims to test, not as terms.
- Buyer names are uneven. Siemens and BBVA appear only on EcoForest’s site. Carbon2Nature is a real project partner. Cloverly is a listing. There is no public investor round.
- DUA’s own words say avoidance comes first and credits are the residual, tied to assets they control. A partnership that only brokers offsets will undershoot how she describes the business.

### Three questions for the call

1. Which vehicle is the partnership, EcoForest’s credit supply or DUA’s compliance product, and what would she want from REIN Hubs in practical terms?
2. Which benefit-share rule is real (70% or at least 79%), in which contracts, and which states beyond Durango, Chihuahua, and Jalisco are actually in development?
3. Who runs DUA day to day (Roxana Rosas or Rossana Reynoso), what is live versus announced, and is there any plan outside Mexico?
