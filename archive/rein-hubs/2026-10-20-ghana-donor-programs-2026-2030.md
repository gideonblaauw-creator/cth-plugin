# Ghana donor programs, 2026 to 2030

Internal research for the REIN Ghana hub. Not added to the Laurence one-pager. Not for circulation.

Question: which donor-sponsored programs are planned or still running in Ghana from 2026 to 2030 on climate, entrepreneurship, innovation, or green jobs, and could a university-hosted climate-startup hub (about US$500,000 to US$1 million, multi-year) get funding or a partnership.

Checked: GIZ/BMZ, EU Global Gateway / NDICI Ghana MIP, FCDO, Global Affairs Canada, Danida, SECO, the Dutch embassy (Orange Corners), AfDB, World Bank, UNDP, Mastercard Foundation, the Green Climate Fund, and JICA. Amounts below are copied from the linked public page. A US dollar conversion is not supplied unless the source gives one. UNVERIFIED means the page does not say it.

## US aid

A March 2025 Appropriations letter, filed in federal court, states that 86 percent of USAID awards were terminated. POLITICO reported that the review ended more than 5,300 USAID grants and contracts worth more than US$27 billion. Do not treat USAID as a live source for a new Ghana hub grant. What, if anything, the State Department kept for Ghana climate or entrepreneurship work is UNVERIFIED.

## Inventory

### GIZ / BMZ

- Program: Support to the Private and Financial Sector (PFS), including the Business Incubation and Acceleration Programme with the Ghana Climate Innovation Centre at Ashesi University.
- Period: PFS is 01/2023 to 12/2025. It does not, on the published sheet, run through 2030. A 2026 extension is UNVERIFIED.
- Budget: EUR 10.1 million from BMZ, plus EUR 1.7 million EU co-financing for vaccine production. The GCIC partnership was announced at about EUR 1.2 million. Participating businesses can receive up to EUR 10,000 each.
- Focus: MSME employment, green jobs, green incubation.
- Hub or startup path: This is the closest public match to a university climate incubator. The partnership itself is the right shape and size. Whether a second partnership is open in 2026 is UNVERIFIED.
- https://www.giz.de/en/downloads/giz2023-en-ghana-private-and-finance-sector-pfs.pdf
- https://ghanacic.ashesi.edu.gh/giz-partners-with-ashesis-gcic-towards-green-entrepreneurship-development/

### EU, Global Gateway / NDICI Ghana MIP

- Program: Joint Programming Ghana 2021 to 2027. Priority 1 is green growth for jobs. Team Europe includes the EU, EIB, and several member states.
- Period: The joint document runs 2021 to 2027. The euro table in that document is for 2021 to 2024 only. The 2025 to 2027 allocation is subject to a later EU decision. That later amount is UNVERIFIED.
- Budget: EUR 203 million indicative for 2021 to 2024, of which EUR 71.05 million (35 percent) is Priority 1, green growth for jobs. The Commission country page also says the EU has allocated EUR 339 million to the partnership for 2021 to 2027. The MIP annex does not break out 2025 to 2027, so do not treat EUR 339 million as a confirmed remaining balance.
- Focus: Green and circular businesses, green jobs, skills, energy transition, sustainable cities.
- Hub or startup path: The priority text supports green enterprise and skills. A named grant window for a university hub is UNVERIFIED. The Joint Innovation Facility (Africa-Europe, Global Gateway related) offered EUR 100,000 to EUR 250,000, with Ghana eligible. That ticket is below US$500,000 to US$1 million, and the cited call closed in August 2025.
- https://international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu/system/files/2022-01/mip-2021-c2021-9368-ghana-annex_en.pdf
- https://international-partnerships.ec.europa.eu/countries/ghana_en
- https://techtrends.africa/apply-now-joint-innovation-facility-offers-up-to-e250k-for-african-led-innovations/

### FCDO

- Program: UK-Ghana Growth Partnership, 2026 to 2028. Separate from TRANSFORM, which gives about GBP 100,000 per impact enterprise.
- Period: 2026 to 2028 for the Growth Partnership.
- Budget: The GOV.UK release does not give one total for the partnership. Named pieces include a GBP 5 million ODA Green Project Preparation Facility, and GBP 6 million for science and technology collaboration that includes the Ghana AI Strategy. Other figures in that release are commercial investments, not hub grants (GBP 101 million ship repair, a planned GBP 85 million reforestation fund, GBP 9 million forest restoration).
- Focus: Private investment, trade, infrastructure, skills, and some climate-aligned project preparation.
- Hub or startup path: The GBP 5 million facility prepares climate infrastructure projects. It is not a startup-hub grant. A university role on skills or AI is plausible and UNVERIFIED. TRANSFORM remains too small for a US$500,000 to US$1 million hub.
- https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-ghana-growth-partnership-to-drive-jobs-investment-and-skills
- https://www.graphic.com.gh/news/general-news/ghana-news-uk-govt-partners-present-gbp500-000-grant-to-5-start-ups.html

### Global Affairs Canada

- Program: Supporting the Green Economy of Ghana, through the Ghana Climate Innovation Centre at Ashesi University.
- Period: 23 August 2021 to 31 March 2026. Only the first quarter of 2026 is inside this window. A follow-on is UNVERIFIED.
- Budget: Maximum contribution $10,000,000 on the Canada project browser. The page does not write "CAD". It is a Canadian government page, so read it as Canadian dollars. A US dollar conversion is UNVERIFIED.
- Focus: Climate entrepreneurship for small and medium firms, including proof-of-concept grants through GCIC.
- Hub or startup path: This is a published multi-year grant to a Ghanaian university for a climate entrepreneurship centre. The envelope is larger than US$500,000 to US$1 million. It is nearly finished.
- https://w05.international.gc.ca/projectbrowser-banqueprojets/project-projet/details/p008970001

### Danida

- Program: Danida Green Business Partnerships, 2025 application round.
- Period: Full projects last 3 to 5 years. A project approved from the 2025 round can still be running in 2028 to 2030. The concept-note deadline was 22 April 2025. A later round is UNVERIFIED.
- Budget: DKK 4 million to DKK 15 million per full project. The programme document puts the whole engagement at DKK 416 million. Published in kroner, not US dollars. A conversion is UNVERIFIED. The band is wide enough that a US$500,000 to US$1 million project can sit inside it.
- Focus: Climate adaptation and resilience, with emphasis on water and food systems. Ghana is an eligible country.
- Hub or startup path: Needs an international commercial partner and a non-commercial administrative partner. The 2025 guidelines say public institutions and universities are not eligible as the administrative partner. ACity could not hold the grant under those rules. A partner role beside an eligible administrator is UNVERIFIED.
- https://danida-business-partnerships.dk/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/DGBP-2025-Guidelines-read-these-if-you-want-to-apply.pdf
- https://danida-business-partnerships.dk/dgbp-2025-is-now-open-to-receive-applications/

### SECO

- Program: Ghana Private Sector Competitiveness Programme II (GPSCP II).
- Period: January 2023 to December 2027.
- Budget: Contract value CHF 9,000,000 (NIRAS project page, donor SECO).
- Focus: Cashew and oil palm value chains, policy reform, and a private-sector partnership window. Not a climate-startup hub.
- Hub or startup path: A university climate hub is a poor fit. Whether a hub could use the private-sector window is UNVERIFIED.
- https://www.niras.com/projects/ghana-private-sector-competitiveness-programme/

### Netherlands, Orange Corners

- Program: Orange Corners Ghana, with the Orange Corners Innovation Fund, and the separate OCIAC subsidy for incubation hosts.
- Period: The Ghana acceleration project on the RVO database ran 1 July 2019 to 10 March 2025, budget EUR 388,297. OCIF rules cited here cover grants to 31 December 2025. OCIAC host projects can run 5 years. The round described on the RVO page as open was for Benin, not Ghana. A Ghana host round for 2026 to 2030 is UNVERIFIED.
- Budget: OCIF Track II for Ghana is up to EUR 50,000 per entrepreneur (part loan, part grant). Track I is up to EUR 5,000 per incubatee. EUR 1.4 million was the location ceiling in the 2021 to 2025 OCIF rules. OCIAC allows up to EUR 750,000 per target location for a host, at up to 75 percent of project cost.
- Focus: Youth entrepreneurship. Climate is not the core theme. The embassy has pointed the fund at horticulture and cocoa SMEs.
- Hub or startup path: The per-startup ticket is below the REIN band. The EUR 750,000 host subsidy is inside the band, but it is not an open Ghana call on the page checked.
- https://english.rvo.nl/sites/default/files/2023-07/Unofficial-Translation-OCIF-Administrative-Rules-2021-2025.pdf
- https://english.rvo.nl/subsidies-financing/ociac
- https://projects.rvo.nl/projects/nl-kvk-27378529-poc19gh01
- https://www.orangecorners.com/country/ghana/

### AfDB

- Program: MicroGREEN, Strengthening Women, Youth and People with Disabilities' Micro-Entrepreneurship for Green Jobs, financed by the Youth Entrepreneurship and Innovation Multi-Donor Trust Fund and the Fund for African Private Sector Assistance.
- Period: Listed as March 2024 on the YEI portfolio, implemented over two years in the April 2024 release. The FAPA agreement is dated 11 August 2025. An end date past 2026 is UNVERIFIED.
- Budget: April 2024 AfDB release: US$999,000 YEI MDTF grant for Ghana and Senegal. August 2025 AfDB release: US$850,000 FAPA grant, and it calls the earlier YEI commitment an initial US$1 million. Use the figure that matches the page you cite. The two wordings differ by US$1,000.
- Focus: Green jobs in agroforestry, fisheries, and biodiversity for women, youth, and people with disabilities.
- Hub or startup path: Right theme and right ticket size. The implementer is Invest in Africa, not a university. A new grant to Academic City is UNVERIFIED.
- https://www.afdb.org/en/news-and-events/press-releases/african-development-bank-and-invest-africa-sign-850000-grant-agreement-boost-inclusive-green-jobs-ghana-and-senegal-85980
- https://afdb.africa-newsroom.com/press/ghanaian-and-senegalese-entrepreneurs-to-benefit-from-african-development-bank-youth-entrepreneurship-and-innovation-multidonor-trust-fund-yei-mdtf-grant-for-green-jobs-in-natural-resources?lang=en

### World Bank

- Program: Ghana Jobs and Skills Project (P166996).
- Period: Approved 2020, effective January 2021, closing date 30 June 2026. Only the first half of 2026 remains.
- Budget: US$200 million IDA credit. Component 2, entrepreneurship and micro and small enterprise support, is US$100 million. Within that, the October 2024 aide-memoire splits US$40 million for entrepreneurship training and startup grants to individuals, and US$60 million for competitive grants to private enterprises. Disbursed US$133.8 million as of October 2024.
- Focus: Apprenticeship, entrepreneurship, and labour-market systems. Climate appears in the training content, not as a separate climate-hub window.
- Hub or startup path: Grants run through government agencies (GEA / NBSSI and partners). An open call for a university hub is not in the project paper. A training-provider role is UNVERIFIED.
- https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2020/06/30/world-bank-supports-jobs-skills-development-and-digital-transformation-in-ghana
- https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099122424083531231/txt/P166996-ead5f40d-f8d6-4cc1-b3ea-9de894ee58e2.txt

No separate World Bank climate-innovation operation for a Ghana startup hub, with a 2026 to 2030 budget, was found in this pass. That absence is not proof none exists.

### UNDP

- Program: Climate Promise, energy and investment workstreams, Ghana.
- Period: The project page describes a one-year cycle through December 2024. A continuation through 2030 is UNVERIFIED.
- Budget: US$864,000 total project budget. Ten low-value grants to NGOs and trade associations. Individual grant sizes are not published on that page.
- Focus: NDC support, clean cooking, energy efficiency, waste, renewable-energy investment plans.
- Hub or startup path: The budget is below a US$500,000 to US$1 million hub grant, and the grants are described as low-value. Not a fit for the REIN ask.
- https://www.undp.org/ghana/projects/climate-promise-energy-and-investment-work-streams

### Mastercard Foundation

- Program A: BRIDGE-in-Agriculture, under Young Africa Works. Lead implementer CrossBoundary.
- Period: June 2023 to June 2028.
- Budget: The Foundation page says a USD 117 million partnership. CrossBoundary separately says a target of US$87 million lent to SMEs by 2028. These are different figures. Do not add them.
- Focus: Affordable capital and skills for agriculture SMEs, youth, and women in Ghana.
- Hub or startup path: Live and large, but the path is banks and agri-SMEs, not a climate-tech university hub. A hub grant is UNVERIFIED.
- https://mastercardfdn.org/en/what-we-do/our-programs/building-resilience-and-investing-in-the-development-and-growth-of-entrepreneurs-in-agriculture-bridge-in-agriculture/
- https://crossboundary.com/work/bridge-in-agriculture-mastercard-foundation/

- Program B: Climate Impact Challenge Fund, inside the Fund for Resilience and Prosperity.
- Period: The parent fund is described as seven years. Round 2 applications ran 13 October 2025 to 30 January 2026. Grants are disbursed over three years, so an award can run into 2029.
- Budget: Grants of US$250,000 to US$1,500,000 over three years. The parent fund is US$126 million across 20 African countries, not a Ghana envelope.
- Focus: Climate-impact SMEs. Ghana is a focus country.
- Hub or startup path: The ticket covers US$500,000 to US$1 million for a for-profit SME with at least three years of operations. It does not fund the university host. Cohort firms could apply. A hub grant is UNVERIFIED.
- https://frp.org/sites/default/files/2024-11/Climate%20Impact%20Challenge%20Fund_summary.pdf

- Program C: Young Africa Works MSME program with Absa in Ghana is listed as October 2020 to September 2025. It does not extend into this window on that page.
- https://mastercardfdn.org/en/what-we-do/our-programs/young-africa-works-msme-program/

### Green Climate Fund

- Program A: FP265, Climate-resilient landscapes for sustainable livelihoods in northern Ghana. Accredited entity UNEP.
- Period: Approved 3 July 2025. Estimated completion 3 July 2032.
- Budget: GCF grant US$63,211,141. Total project value about US$70.2 million. Co-financing on the project page totals US$6,987,681.
- Focus: Adaptation for smallholders in northern Ghana: agriculture, water, landscapes. Not a startup accelerator.
- Hub or startup path: Public-sector project. A grant to Academic City from this approval is not available. A partnership is UNVERIFIED and unlikely to match the REIN model.
- https://www.greenclimate.fund/portfolio/projects/fp265

- Program B: FP231, Accelerating Solar Action Program (ASAP). Accredited entity Ecobank. Ghana.
- Period: Approved 6 March 2024. Estimated completion 17 January 2038.
- Budget: Total project value US$31.6 million on the GCF project page. The GCF-only share was not copied in this pass. Treat any split other than the total as UNVERIFIED.
- Focus: Distributed solar for MSMEs, households, and agricultural cooperatives.
- Hub or startup path: Could matter for energy startups that need finance. It is not a grant to a university hub.
- https://www.greenclimate.fund/portfolio/projects/fp231

### JICA

- Program: Project NINJA (Next Innovation with Japan), with a Ghana incubation cohort alongside Ghana Enterprises Agency and Accra Digital Centre.
- Period: Global programme launched January 2020, with a stated aim to support 1,600 companies by 2030. The Ghana cohort with published grants is 2022. A Ghana budget for 2026 to 2030 is UNVERIFIED.
- Budget: Ghana cohort: GH₵240,000 in total, up to GH₵30,000 per startup. Far below US$500,000 to US$1 million.
- Focus: Startup incubation. Sectors in that cohort included agriculture, waste, and software, not a climate-hub mandate.
- Hub or startup path: Useful as a partner for training. Not a source for the hub's cash need on the published ticket.
- https://www.jica.go.jp/english/activities/issues/private_sec/__icsFiles/afieldfile/2025/07/25/ProjectNINJA_english_250725rev_1.pdf

## Top 10 by fit for a REIN Ghana hub

Fit means: Ghana, still touching 2026 to 2030, theme close to climate entrepreneurship, and a plausible path to about US$500,000 to US$1 million over more than one year for a university hub or its startups. Rank is a judgment on those tests, not a score the donors published.

1. Danida Green Business Partnerships. Best published grant band (DKK 4 million to 15 million, 3 to 5 years) and Ghana is eligible for climate adaptation. The 2025 call is closed, a university cannot administer the grant, and a commercial partner is required.
2. GIZ / BMZ with GCIC at Ashesi. Best proof that a donor will fund a Ghana university climate incubator, at about EUR 1.2 million. The parent PFS programme ends in December 2025, so a 2026 renewal is UNVERIFIED.
3. AfDB MicroGREEN (YEI MDTF and FAPA). US$999,000 or the later "US$1 million" wording, plus US$850,000, for green entrepreneurship in Ghana and Senegal. Already awarded to Invest in Africa. A new university grant is UNVERIFIED.
4. Mastercard Foundation Climate Impact Challenge Fund. US$250,000 to US$1,500,000 over three years, Ghana included, awards can run past 2026. For cohort SMEs, not for Academic City as host.
5. EU NDICI / Global Gateway, green growth for jobs, through 2027. Largest strategic window. The 2025 to 2027 euro amount is not set in the MIP. A hub-sized call is UNVERIFIED.
6. UK-Ghana Growth Partnership, 2026 to 2028. Live, with a GBP 5 million green project-preparation facility and GBP 6 million for AI and science partnerships. Neither line is a startup-hub grant. A university partnership is UNVERIFIED.
7. Mastercard Foundation BRIDGE-in-Agriculture, to June 2028. USD 117 million partnership (Foundation page). Real money and youth enterprise, but agriculture finance through banks, not a climate-tech hub.
8. Global Affairs Canada, GCIC at Ashesi. The right institution and a $10,000,000 envelope, but the project ends 31 March 2026. Follow-on UNVERIFIED.
9. World Bank Ghana Jobs and Skills. US$200 million, with US$100 million on entrepreneurship, but it closes 30 June 2026 and the channel is government, not an open university call.
10. GCF ASAP (FP231), to 2038. Total project value US$31.6 million for solar finance to MSMEs. A path for energy startups, not for the hub operator.

Not ranked for this ask: UNDP Climate Promise (US$864,000, and the published cycle ended in 2024), JICA NINJA (GH₵30,000 per startup, no Ghana 2026 budget found), Orange Corners (up to EUR 50,000 per entrepreneur, Ghana host round not open on the page checked), FCDO TRANSFORM (about GBP 100,000 per firm), EU Joint Innovation Facility (EUR 100,000 to EUR 250,000), SECO GPSCP II (CHF 9 million, cashew and oil palm), and USAID.
