# Brand rules (Datalab and Observatorio de la Tierra)

Short pointer for desks and Hands. Full deck contract: `docs/html-deck-protocol.md` (Download PDF, Datalab, EO, shared sections).

## Datalab

- Logo: Option A datagrid master (`brand/datalab-brandbook-2026-11/assets/logos/`). Option B (layers) secondary only.
- Naming: Datalab; endorsement "Datalab powered by CleantechHUB" (HUB caps).
- Visuals: CTH five colours; Titillium Web titles; Open Sans body; flat fills; no em dashes; datagrid logo on every top bar.
- Datalab top bar: the light-blue partner box shows only the EO logo, with no CTH or Externado logos (Datalab is embeddable in client environments).
- Sandboxes: Open Energy / Open Air / Open Water Sandbox (planned); health room "Clean Air for Health Sandbox" (ES: "Sandbox Aire Limpio para la Salud").

## Observatorio de la Tierra (EO v3)

- Source: Propuesta técnica v3; `brand/earth-observatory/out/observatorio-de-la-tierra-brand-book-v3.pdf`.
- Layout: deep-blue cover, orbit lines, white inner pages, light-green section numbers, blue table headers, light callouts; tagline "Observa. Actúa. Transforma."; logo row Externado | CleantechHUB | Observatorio de la Tierra.
- Avoid: v1 full-bleed gradients, v2 Tierra natural, stock unrelated photos, colour blocks over text.

## Shared
