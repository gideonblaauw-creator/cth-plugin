2. Reframe: OPEX, not CAPEX alone

  • Contraloría: sustainability concern where methodologies to pay for operation, microgrid administration, and off-grid PV are missing; maintenance and monitoring failures.3
  • Amazonas: ~COP 30 billion (royalties) solar investment not operating; damaged batteries — “critical state” on sustainability.4
  • CREG: thousands of rural PV systems without management → “often abandoned”; missing standards, certified installers, and O&M.5

3. The cooperative as the tool

CTH proposal

Community O&M + revenue + local ownership (energy cooperative) complements CAPEX with explicit OPEX contracts (PV, batteries, monitoring; CREG/SSPD) and community governance; IMPE access/quality KPIs — does not replace ICEE/IPEM.
