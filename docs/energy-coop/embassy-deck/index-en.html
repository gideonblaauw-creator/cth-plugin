CleantechHUB — overview

Climate innovation hub for Latin America para América Latina: conecta startups cleantech con corporativos, inversores, multilaterales y gobierno.

  • Since 2019 impulsando emprendimiento climático y cleantech (cleantechhub.net).
  • Countries cited on website: Colombia, Perú, El Salvador, Guatemala, Costa Rica.
  • Platforms: institutional website, wiki (BookStack), portafolio Nexus, decks y demos públicos.

Source: JSON-LD Organization, cleantechhub.net (oct 2026).
