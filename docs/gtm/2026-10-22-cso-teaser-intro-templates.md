# CSO gap snapshot — intro templates (LinkedIn + email)

**Date:** 2026-10-22  
**Author voice:** Gideon Blaauw, first person  
**Status:** Draft only. Human-in-the-loop before any send. Do not deploy or transmit from this file.

**What a gap snapshot is:** A short Datalab read of a company’s sustainability report: one priority where declared progress still leaves room to move, plus Colombian cleantech startups that might fit. Teaser points to the snapshot; it is conversation material, not an audit or a commercial pitch.

**Placeholders (use exactly):** `{first_name}`, `{company}`, `{teaser_url}`, `{top_gap}`

**Spanish register:** Colombian Spanish, **tú** in a neutral professional tone (direct, warm; no hype).
