02 · Naming and logo system

02 · Nombre y sistema de logo

Naming and endorsement

Nombre y respaldo

Master brand string (exact): Datalab powered by CleantechHUB. Always spell Datalab as one word in lockups, headers, and endorsement lines. CleantechHUB stays one word with capital H-U-B. Do not write "Data Lab" for the brand name.

Nombre principal (exacto): Datalab powered by CleantechHUB. Escribir siempre Datalab en una palabra. CleantechHUB se mantiene como una palabra con H-U-B mayúsculas.

Endorsement clear space: gap between wordmark baseline and endorsement cap height = at least 0.5× endorsement cap height; clear space below endorsement = at least 1× endorsement cap height. Do not place other type or logos inside that zone.

Endorsement minimum size: 11 px / 8 pt cap height for the endorsement line (Open Sans Semibold). If the line would fall below that, switch to the without-endorsement lockup or scale the full lockup up.

Mark clear space: 1× mark height on all sides. Minimum lockup width: 120 px (digital), 32 mm (print) for master without endorsement; add 24 px height when endorsement is present.
