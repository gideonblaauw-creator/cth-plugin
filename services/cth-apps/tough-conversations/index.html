After the conversation
Tough conversations leave a physical trace — adrenaline and cortisol don't just vanish when you hang up. Block 15 minutes after. Walk, stretch, step outside, breathe. Don't jump straight into your next task. You've earned the buffer — and your body needs it to come back down.

for tough conversations

Tough Conversations helps you prepare for the talks you'd rather not have — and have them better than you thought you could.

How it works
1

Describe your situation

You share the context of the conversation you're preparing for — what makes it hard, what you're worried about. No names needed. The more specific you are, the more useful the support.

2

Choose where you need help most

Pick the area that feels most pressing: calming yourself before the call, preparing both sides of the picture, handling the conversation in the moment, or getting to a clear outcome at the end.

3

Get a personalised technique

The toolkit selects an evidence-based framework and uses Claude (Anthropic's AI) to apply it specifically to your situation — explaining what the technique is, why it works, and giving you words or actions you can actually use.

4

Keep exploring, then save your kit

Ask for more examples, try another technique, or switch focus. Every tip is collected in your session. When you're ready, print or download a personalised Help Kit PDF.

About this tool

Tough Conversations is built on a framework designed to support the full arc of a difficult conversation — from the moment you dread it to the moment it ends. The four-area structure draws on research from the Harvard Negotiation Project, the NeuroLeadership Institute, Nonviolent Communication, and Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction.

This is a browser-based application. Your situation is not stored. It is sent temporarily to Claude (Anthropic's AI) to generate your personalised advice, then discarded. No account required.
