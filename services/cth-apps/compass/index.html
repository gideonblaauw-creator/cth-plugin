🧪 A first experiment — and we want your help to improve it

Compass is our first experiment with gamification to make it easier and more fun to discover the many dimensions of building a great organisation, and to get a first sense of your priorities. We think it works best when done as a team — try it together and see where your instincts converge.

It's also our first experiment with Claude as an organisational development advisor. We're curious how useful it feels to you in practice — and where it falls short.
