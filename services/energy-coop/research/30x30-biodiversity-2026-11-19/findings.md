## 1. What 30×30 is — and Colombia’s line of march

### 1.1 Global Target 3 (Kunming–Montreal GBF)

**Target 3** (“30×30”) requires that by **2030** at least **30%** of terrestrial and inland water areas **and** marine/coastal areas be **effectively conserved and managed** through representative, connected, **equitably governed** systems of **protected areas** and **other effective area-based conservation measures (OECMs)**, respecting Indigenous and local community rights ([CBD Target 3](https://www.cbd.int/gbf/targets/3)).

The full GBF decision text repeats the same Target 3 language ([COP-15 Decision 15/4 (PDF)](https://www.cbd.int/doc/decisions/cop-15/cop-15-dec-04-en.pdf)).

### 1.2 Colombia: NBSAP / Plan de Acción 2024–2030 and COP16 (Cali, 2024)

Colombia presented its **Plan de Acción de Biodiversidad al 2030** on **21 October 2024** at **COP16** in Cali as host country ([MinAmbiente, 21 Oct 2024](https://www.minambiente.gov.co/colombia-abre-la-cop16-con-la-presentacion-oficial-de-su-plan-de-accion-para-proteger-la-biodiversidad-al-2030/)). The press release states:

- The plan has **four strategic bets**, **six national goals**, and **191 actions**, aligned to the GBF’s 23 targets ([same source](https://www.minambiente.gov.co/colombia-abre-la-cop16-con-la-presentacion-oficial-de-su-plan-de-accion-para-proteger-la-biodiversidad-al-2030/)).
- A key goal is to move from **24%** of national territory under environmental protection measures today to **34%** for terrestrial, inland water, and marine-coastal areas ([same source](https://www.minambiente.gov.co/colombia-abre-la-cop16-con-la-presentacion-oficial-de-su-plan-de-accion-para-proteger-la-biodiversidad-al-2030/)).

The CBD national clearing-house summary (**Goal 5**) states that by 2030 Colombia will reach **34%** conservation and management of terrestrial, inland water, and coastal marine areas through **protected areas, OECMs (OMEC), and Indigenous, Afro-descendant, and peasant territories** ([Colombia CHM — national goals](https://co.chm-cbd.net/en/objetivosnacionales)).

**UNDP / PNUD role in NBSAP update:** The clearing-house notes GEF funding **“Support to early actions of the Global Biodiversity Framework”** with **UNDP implementation** in synergy with TNC Colombia, GIZ, and other partners ([Colombia CHM](https://co.chm-cbd.net/en/objetivosnacionales)). The official **Plan de Acción para la Biodiversidad en Colombia 2030** PDF credits **Proyecto GEF Early Actions Support, NBSAP. Programa de las Naciones Unidas para el Desarrollo** ([PDF, Oct 2024](https://www.minambiente.gov.co/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Plan-Accion-Biodiversidad-Colombia-2030.pdf)).

**UNDP BIOFIN — finance gap (adjacent to implementation, not a conservation programme):** Joint MinAmbiente–PNUD (BIOFIN) assessment estimates roughly **COP 98.95 billones (USD 24.150 millones)** for 2025–2030 and an **~82% financing gap** ([MinAmbiente/PNUD PDF, 1 Jun 2026](https://www.minambiente.gov.co/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/PLAN-ACCION-BIODIVERSIDAD_PNUD_01062026_compressed-1.pdf)). The UNDP Colombia HTML publication URL returned **403/404** during verification — figures are cited from the Ministry-hosted PDF only.

### 1.3 Herencia Colombia (HeCo) — Colombia’s flagship 30×30 finance vehicle

**What it is:** **Herencia Colombia** is a **Project Finance for Permanence (PFP)** launched **23 June 2022**, securing **USD 245 million** (stage one) to support long-term conservation of **32 million hectares** (~16% of Colombia), led by **MinAmbiente** and **Parques Nacionales Naturales de Colombia (PNNC)** with WWF and other partners ([WWF press release, 23 Jun 2022](https://www.worldwildlife.org/news/press-releases/colombia-wwf-and-partners-announce-245m-agreement-to-permanently-protect-vital-systems-of-nations-protected-areas/)).

**30×30 link (WWF Colombia):** WWF Colombia states HeCo advances Colombia’s **30×30** target (30% land and 30% seas by 2030), including creation of new terrestrial and marine protected areas over the first 10 years, and mentions improving **livelihoods of communities in ecological corridors** associated with protected areas ([WWF Colombia — Herencia Colombia](https://www.wwf.org.co/que_hacemos/areas_protegidas/herencia_colombia/)).

**Geography (mosaics):** MinAmbiente lists five HeCo mosaics: **Caribe**, **Corazón Amazonía**, **Transición Orinoquía**, **Andes Centrales**, **Pacífico – Marino Costero**, plus marine priorities (e.g. Cordillera Beata, Pacific seamounts) ([MinAmbiente — Herencia Colombia hub](https://www.minambiente.gov.co/herencia-colombia/)).

**Donors listed by Colombia (no Netherlands named):** MinAmbiente’s donor list includes EU, FAO, GCF, KfW, GEF/World Bank, and research institutes — **not the Netherlands** ([Sobre Herencia Colombia](https://www.minambiente.gov.co/herencia-colombia/sobre-herencia-colombia/)).

**UNDP/World Bank/GEF link (technical, not HeCo cheque-writer):** The Amazon Sustainable Landscapes (ASL) programme documentation describes ASL support to **finalize HeCo design, closing conditions, and transition-fund capitalization**; ASL supervision involves **World Bank, WWF, and UNDP** ([World Bank PFP guide (PDF)](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/438031638766355288/pdf/Securing-Sustainable-Financing-for-Conservation-Areas-A-Guide-to-Project-Finance-for-Permanence.pdf)). HeCo’s **carbon-tax co-financing** and transition fund are described in PNNC’s **Informe HeCo 2023** ([PDF](https://www.parquesnacionales.gov.co/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Informe-HeCo-2023-FINAL-01.04.2024.pdf)).

**GCF–WWF implementation window:** GCF and WWF signed agreement for **“Heritage Colombia, Maximising the Contributions…”** with stated **total project value USD 145.2 million** including **USD 43 million GCF grant**, emphasizing protected-area climate resilience and **sustainable livelihoods for local communities** ([GCF news](https://www.greenclimate.fund/about/news-and-updates/gcf-and-world-wildlife-fund-sign-key-colombia-landscapes-agreement)).

### 4.1 Why 30×30 matters for Energy Coop

30×30 implementation expands **protected areas, OECMs, and territorios étnicos** with emphasis on **equitable governance** and **sustainable use consistent with conservation** ([CBD Target 3](https://www.cbd.int/gbf/targets/3)). Colombia’s **34% Goal 5** explicitly blends **AP, OECM, and Indigenous/Afro/peasant territories** ([CHM Goal 5](https://co.chm-cbd.net/en/objetivosnacionales)). Energy access in **ZNI buffer zones and high-biodiversity departments** is both a **livelihood / diesel substitution** problem and a **governance** problem (who decides siting, benefit-sharing, O&M).
