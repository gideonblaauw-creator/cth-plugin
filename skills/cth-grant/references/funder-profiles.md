# Funder Profiles — CTH Grant History

Quick-reference profiles for every funder CTH has worked with or applied to. Read this when evaluating a new opportunity from a known funder or when you need background for a proposal narrative.

## Tier 1 — Funders CTH Has Won Grants From

### P4G (Partnering for Green Growth)
- **Managed by**: World Resources Institute (WRI)
- **Focus**: Public-private partnerships for SDGs in developing countries
- **Grant size**: ~$200K per partnership round
- **CTH history**: Won R4 (~$200K, 2024–Mar 2026, completed). Rejected R5 (Jan 2026, salary costs at 60%).
- **Key requirements**: Named partnership (min 2 orgs), cost share documentation, strict budget discipline, bi-annual reporting
- **What they care about most**: Partnership quality, operational cost ratio, SDG outcomes, scalability
- **Budget rule**: Use their template exactly. Keep salaries well under their operational threshold.
- **Contact cadence**: Quarterly updates during active grant. Annual touch-base between rounds.

### UNDP Adaptation Fund (AFCIA Program)
- **Managed by**: UNDP Colombia
- **Focus**: Climate adaptation innovation in the Global South
- **Grant size**: $44K–$88K per round
- **CTH history**: Won R1 (~$80K, 2022–2023, completed). Won R2 ($88K, 2024–2025, completed with LVGA report).
- **Key requirements**: FSR format reporting, transaction lists, separate bank account preferred, M&E with baseline
- **What they care about most**: Local ownership, adaptation outcomes, gender inclusion, reporting discipline
- **Budget rule**: FSR format. Banco de Occidente account used for R1/R2.
- **Note**: Both rounds were renewals — CTH's strongest funder relationship. Terminal evaluation exists as a reference.

### Climate KIC / ClimateLaunchpad
- **Managed by**: EIT Climate-KIC (European)
- **Focus**: Climate entrepreneurship competitions and bootcamps
- **Grant size**: EUR 6K–30K depending on country scope
- **CTH history**: CLP 2026 active across Peru (Bank of America, EUR 6K) + Colombia/CA (Irish Aid, 5 countries).
- **Key requirements**: Light reporting, event-based budgets, university hub partnerships
- **What they care about most**: Reach (number of applicants/participants), quality of bootcamp delivery, national lead commitment
- **Budget rule**: Simple. Bootcamp costs, travel, prizes, coordination fees.

### GIZ (CATAL1.5°T)
- **Managed by**: GIZ Mexico
- **Focus**: Climate technology transfer, 1.5°C aligned initiatives
- **Grant size**: ~$30K (CTH's engagement)
- **CTH history**: Direct invitation 2025, ~$30K. Also continued as evaluator.
- **Key requirements**: German procurement standards, detailed unit-cost budgets
- **What they care about most**: Technical expertise, regional knowledge, evaluator credibility

### Pvblic Foundation / REIN Hubs
- **Type**: US-based philanthropic foundation
- **Focus**: Regional innovation ecosystem networks
- **Grant size**: $25K (current agreement)
- **CTH history**: $10K disbursed Apr 2026, $15K pending. Active partnership.
- **Key requirements**: Light reporting, milestone-based disbursement
- **What they care about most**: Network expansion, hub quality, geographic diversity
- **Note**: ⚠️ Never cite REIN Hubs dollar amounts publicly — not confirmed for public disclosure. Use "5 países · 1,000+ ventures" framing instead.

## Tier 2 — Active Pipeline / Pending

### DGGF / Triple Jump
- **Managed by**: Dutch Good Growth Fund, via Triple Jump (fund manager)
- **Focus**: SME finance and growth in developing countries
- **Grant size**: ~EUR 200K (estimated)
- **CTH history**: Proposal submitted 2024–2025. SCBD follow-up completed Jun 2025. Staff turnover at Triple Jump (Julia → Tate). Still in pipeline.
- **Key requirements**: Financial sustainability focus, investment fund logic (not just project budgets), impact measurement
- **What they care about most**: Financial viability, scalability, systematic impact data
