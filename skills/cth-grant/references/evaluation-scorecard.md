---

## Scoring Guide

### Score 5 — Excellent fit
The criterion is a perfect match. CTH has proven capability, existing data, and no constraints.

### Score 4 — Strong fit
Good alignment with minor gaps that are easily addressed (e.g., partner confirmed but needs updated LOI).

### Score 3 — Adequate fit
Workable but requires effort (e.g., co-financing needs to be sourced, budget needs restructuring).

### Score 2 — Weak fit
Significant gap that requires mitigation strategy. Flag this in the CONDITIONAL GO presentation.

### Score 1 — Poor fit / blocker
Fundamental mismatch or dependency that cannot be resolved. Triggers NO-GO.
