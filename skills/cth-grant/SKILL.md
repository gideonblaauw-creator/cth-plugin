# CTH Grant Lifecycle

## Go / No-Go Assessment

Before investing time in any grant application, run this checklist:

### Alignment Check
- Does the call align with CTH's mission (cleantech acceleration, climate innovation)?
- Does CTH have track record in the thematic area?
- Is the geographic focus a CTH operating country?
- Is the grant size worth the application effort? (minimum threshold: $25k USD)

### Capacity Check
- Is there bandwidth to write the proposal in time?
- Does CTH have the required co-funding or match?
- Are qualified partners available for consortium requirements?
- Can CTH meet reporting requirements post-award?

### Competitive Check
- Has CTH applied to this funder before?
- What's the estimated success rate?
- Are there incumbent organizations likely to win?

Score: 8+ of 12 = proceed. 5–7 = proceed with caveats. Below 5 = decline.

## Budget Rules

### The 40% Salary Rule
CTH allocates a maximum of 40% of any grant budget to salaries and personnel costs. This is a hard rule based on 4+ years of grant execution. Funders scrutinize personnel costs, and exceeding 40% signals organizational bloat.

### Standard Budget Categories
1. Personnel (40% max)
2. Travel and per diem
3. Equipment and materials
4. Subcontracts / consultants
5. Indirect costs / overhead (varies by funder)
6. Contingency (if allowed, usually 5%)

### Funder-Specific Budget Notes
- P4G: strict co-funding requirements, 50/50 split expected.
- GIZ: detailed milestone-based disbursement.
- BID/IDB: procurement rules for competitive bids, advance up to 20%.
- Climate KIC: lump-sum budget with milestone verification.
- UNDP: FACE forms, HACT audits, strict reporting templates.

## Proposal Structure

### Concept Note (2–5 pages)
1. Problem statement with data
2. Proposed solution
3. Theory of change (brief)
4. Expected outcomes with indicators
5. Budget summary
6. Organization profile

### Full Proposal (10–25 pages)
1. Executive summary
2. Context and problem analysis
3. Theory of change and intervention logic
4. Methodology and activities
5. Results framework (indicators, baselines, targets)
6. Work plan and timeline
7. Organizational capacity
8. Risk analysis and mitigation
9. Sustainability plan
10. Detailed budget with narrative justification

## Pre-Submission Checklist

- All required annexes attached.
- Budget totals match across narrative and budget table.
- All co-funders have signed letters of commitment.
- Proposal reviewed by at least one person besides the author.
- Submission format matches funder requirements.
- Submitted at least 24 hours before deadline.
