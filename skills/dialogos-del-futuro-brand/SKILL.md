# Diálogos del Futuro Brand Guidelines

Apply these rules to every piece of content created for Diálogos del Futuro. This is a CleantechHUB program — CleantechHUB is the convening organization but Diálogos del Futuro has its own distinct visual identity.

## About the Program

Diálogos del Futuro is a CleantechHUB initiative focused on higher education transformation in Colombia. It convenes policy dialogues between universities, government agencies, and industry to modernize technical and technological education (educación técnica y tecnológica). The program brings together diverse stakeholders to shape the future of workforce-relevant education.

### Typography

- **Headlines:** Montserrat Bold.
- **Body:** Source Sans Pro Regular, 16px base size.
- **Accent text:** Montserrat Medium for labels and navigation.
- Fall back to system sans-serif if web fonts are unavailable.

## Tone of Voice

Follow these four principles in all Diálogos del Futuro copy:

1. **Visionary but grounded** — Paint a compelling picture of education's future while anchoring proposals in practical, implementable steps. Avoid abstract idealism without actionable substance.
2. **Inclusive** — The program convenes diverse stakeholders (universities, government, industry, students). Respect institutional traditions while advocating for transformation. Never dismiss existing approaches.
3. **Policy-aware** — Reference Colombian education law, regulatory frameworks, and institutional structures accurately. When citing specific policies (e.g., Ley 30, CONACES, CNA), verify accuracy with the user if uncertain.
4. **Formal Spanish register** — Use "usted" form for all institutional audiences. This is a formal program involving universities and government — the familiar "tú" is not appropriate here.

### Writing Rules

- Always include the accent mark: "Diálogos del Futuro". Never write "Dialogos del Futuro" or "Dialogos Del Futuro".
- Reference the convening format as "diálogos" (dialogues), not "conferencias" (conferences) or "seminarios" (seminars). The program's identity is rooted in dialogue, not one-directional presentation.
- Include CleantechHUB as the convening organization in all materials. Format: "Una iniciativa de CleantechHUB" or "Convocado por CleantechHUB".
- Use full official names for partner institutions on first reference (e.g., "Universidad de los Andes", not "Uniandes"; "Ministerio de Educación Nacional", not "MEN"). Abbreviations are acceptable on subsequent references.
- When listing participating institutions, present them alphabetically or by sector — never imply a hierarchy among partners.

## Content Rules

### Documents and Presentations

- **Header:** Diálogos del Futuro logo or wordmark (left) + document title (right).
- **Footer:** "© [Year] CleantechHUB Foundation — Diálogos del Futuro" + page number.
- Use the purple-blue-orange palette for all visual elements.
- Structure policy documents with an executive summary, context section, dialogue findings, and recommended actions.

### Social Media

- Tone is more formal than other CleantechHUB programs.
- Lead with insights from dialogues, policy implications, or education data.
- Tag participating institutions and speakers when mentioning them.
- Hashtags: #DiálogosDelFuturo #EducaciónSuperior #CleantechHUB + topic-specific.

### Event Materials

- Include the full list of convening partners with logos when space allows.
- Agenda items should be framed as questions for dialogue, not presentation topics.
- Provide bilingual materials (Spanish primary, English secondary) only when international participants are confirmed.

## Quality Checklist

Before delivering any Diálogos del Futuro content, verify:

1. "Diálogos del Futuro" includes the accent mark on the 'a' everywhere.
2. Colors are from the DdF palette (purple/blue/orange), not CleantechHUB palette.
3. Typography uses Montserrat/Source Sans Pro stack.
4. Spanish uses "usted" register throughout.
5. Partner institutions are named with full official names on first reference.
6. CleantechHUB is attributed as the convening organization.
7. Format references use "diálogos", not "conferencias" or "seminarios".
8. Policy references are accurate (or flagged for user verification).
