## Eight core principles (one-liners)

1. Open models and open weights by default for finance, impact, and public-data infrastructure.
2. Security through scrutiny, not secrecy: testable systems beat black boxes.
3. Data sovereignty for Latin American institutions and communities.
4. Verifiable agents: every material action traceable to an accountable human.
5. Tokenized assets backed by auditable data and evidence chains.
6. Climate and impact data treated as critical infrastructure.
7. Fair access so small institutions and the Global South are not priced out.
8. Humans stay accountable; agents assist but do not inherit liability.
