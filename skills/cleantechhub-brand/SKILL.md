# CleantechHUB Brand Communications Skill

This skill ensures all CleantechHUB communications are consistent with the official Brand Book (Manual de Marca, Jan 2024).

---

## Brand Identity at a Glance

**Organization:** CleantechHUB — an inclusive cleantech entrepreneurship ecosystem.
**Tagline:** *Inspira. Actúa. Transforma.*
**Brand DNA:** The theory of change is our reason for being. We believe in people who believe in their own ideas.
**Mission:** Strengthen startups, foster green innovation, and build an inclusive and sustainable future. We focus on the entrepreneur as a catalyst for change.

## Color Palette

Always use ONLY these five official colors. No other colors are permitted.

| Name | HEX | RGB | Role |
|------|-----|-----|------|
| Deep Blue | `#0C498A` | R:12, G:73, B:138 | Primary — official documents, formal comms, logo default |
| Light Cyan | `#B2EEFA` | R:178, G:238, B:250 | Primary — backgrounds, light fills, "water" element |
| Light Green | `#9DC384` | R:157, G:195, B:132 | Primary — "earth" element, creative pieces, secondary emphasis |
| Forest Green | `#669348` | R:102, G:147, B:72 | Secondary — supporting earth tones |
| Sky Blue | `#69B5FA` | R:105, G:181, B:250 | Secondary — supporting water tones |

**Color meaning:**
- Blues → "water as a vital element" / human drive for change
- Greens → "earth we belong to and must care for"

**FORBIDDEN:** Black backgrounds with dark blue logo, purple or magenta backgrounds, any color outside this palette.

## Typography

### Logo Wordmark Font
**Titilium Bold** — used for the "CleantechHUB" wordmark itself.

### Document & Communication Fonts

| Role | Font | Use |
|------|------|-----|
| **Primary** | Open Sans (all weights) | Body text, directions, addresses, main copy in all documents |
| **Secondary** | PT Sans | Accompanying brand copy, supporting text |
| **Tertiary** | Hind Bold | Extra artwork, modern variations |

**For printed documents:** Open Sans Regular, 8pt, left-aligned, automatic line spacing, mixed case (not all-caps for body text).

**Web/digital fallback stack:** `'Open Sans', 'PT Sans', sans-serif`

## Logo Rules

### Approved Versions
1. **Official (Dark Blue, `#0C498A`)** — default for all formal/official documents
2. **Light Cyan (`#B2EEFA`)** — for creative, friendly, less formal contexts
3. **Forest Green (`#669348`)** — important topics, semi-formal, not strictly official
4. **Light Green (`#9DC384`)** — creative, diverse visual language, user-friendly pieces
5. **Black on white / White on black** — only for monochrome reproduction

**Logo orientation:** Both horizontal and vertical layouts are permitted.
**Minimum size:** 4 cm wide (print). Never reproduce smaller.
**Clear space:** 3 cm exclusion zone around the logo on all sides — no text or graphics inside this area.

### Logo Prohibitions
- Do NOT place the logo on any background where it cannot be fully read
- Do NOT use the logo on black backgrounds combined with dark blue (legibility conflict)
- Do NOT use colors outside the official palette for the logo
- Do NOT distort, rotate, recolor, add shadows, or apply effects to the logo
- Do NOT place the logo over busy photographic backgrounds without a solid-color shield/box underneath

### Partnership / Co-branding
When appearing alongside other organizations' logos (events, partnerships), use the **dark blue horizontal logo**. Maintain the same minimum size and clear space rules.

## Layout & Design Principles

### Backgrounds
Use solid blocks of brand colors. The primary visual pattern is **large solid color blocks** — typically a light cyan or sky blue base with a contrasting deep blue or green rectangle overlapping or placed to one side.

### Section Labels
Section numbers and labels use **Open Sans, small, in dark blue** — format: `01 Introducción`, `02 Guías Visuales`, etc.

### Typography Hierarchy in Documents & Slides
- **Large display headline:** Titilium Bold or Open Sans Bold, in brand color (usually deep blue or white on colored bg)
- **Subheadings:** Open Sans Bold or SemiBold, dark blue
- **Body text:** Open Sans Regular, dark blue or dark gray text on light backgrounds
- **Captions/small text:** Open Sans Regular 8pt

### Photography
- Subject matter: nature, people, entrepreneurship, sustainability, community
- Style: warm, optimistic, real people, green environments
- When overlaying text on photos: place text on a **solid brand-color block** over/beside the photo — never directly on a busy image

### Icons & Illustrations
The brand has a custom icon library featuring: rocket ship, globe/earth, plant/flora, water waves, wetland scenes, and similar nature+innovation imagery. Always use in brand colors only. Rounded-corner tile format is the standard icon container.

See **Asset Library** section below for the full list of available files.

## Document Templates

### Printed Letter / Official Document
- **Paper:** A4 / Carta (210 × 297 mm)
- **Header:** CleantechHUB logo (dark blue, horizontal) + tagline "Inspira. Actúa. Transforma." on a wavy deep blue / light cyan header band
- **Body font:** Open Sans Regular 8–10pt, left-aligned
- **Footer:** Company address · Phone · www.cleantechhub.net (small, centered, Open Sans)
- **Signature block:** President's name + "Sign here" line, right-aligned

### Envelope (American DL 220×110 mm)
- Logo + tagline left-aligned
- Address in Open Sans Regular 8pt, left-aligned

### Envelope (C4 Bolsa 229×324 mm)
- Same rules as DL but larger format
- Wavy blue/cyan decorative element on the flap

### Digital / HTML Documents
- Same color palette and typography — load Open Sans from Google Fonts
- Background: light cyan or white
- Headers: deep blue filled rectangles
- Accent blocks: green or sky blue

### Presentations (Slides)
- Background: light cyan (`#B2EEFA`) or white
- Title block: deep blue (`#0C498A`) filled rectangle, white or light cyan text
- Body text: dark blue on light background
- Section dividers: full-bleed color blocks in any palette color
- Logo: top-left corner, horizontal, dark blue version (unless on dark bg, then white/light version)

### Social Media / Flyers
- Bold, large headline text on solid brand-color backgrounds
- Keep compositions clean — 1–2 colors max per piece
- Always include tagline or brand mantra when space allows

## Quick Checklist — Before Producing Any Output

- [ ] Only palette colors used? (`#0C498A`, `#B2EEFA`, `#9DC384`, `#669348`, `#69B5FA`)
- [ ] Font is Open Sans (primary), PT Sans (secondary), or Hind Bold (tertiary)?
- [ ] Logo version matches context (dark blue for formal, green/cyan for creative)?
- [ ] Logo has adequate clear space and is never placed on a problematic background?
- [ ] Tone is inspiring, inclusive, action-oriented?
- [ ] Tagline or brand mantra included where appropriate?
- [ ] Photography/icons stay within brand style (nature + innovation, optimistic)?
