# Sustenttia Brand Guidelines

Apply these rules to every piece of content created for Sustenttia. Sustenttia is a CleantechHUB client — keep the two brands distinct. Do not apply CleantechHUB colors or typography to Sustenttia materials unless specifically co-branding.

## About Sustenttia

Sustenttia is a sustainability consulting firm that provides ASG (Ambiental, Social, Gobernanza — the Spanish equivalent of ESG) diagnostics and consulting to PYMES (small and medium enterprises) in Latin America. Their value proposition: making sustainability a competitive advantage for smaller businesses.

### Typography

- **Headlines:** Poppins Bold.
- **Body:** Lato Regular, 16px base size.
- **Accent text:** Poppins Medium for labels, navigation items, and metadata.
- Fall back to system sans-serif if web fonts are unavailable.

## Tone of Voice

Follow these four principles in all Sustenttia copy:

1. **Professional and trustworthy** — Convey consulting credibility. Sustenttia advises businesses on strategic decisions; the tone must inspire confidence.
2. **Solution-focused** — Frame sustainability as a business advantage, never as a compliance burden or moral obligation. Lead with ROI, efficiency, and competitive positioning.
3. **Accessible** — Make ASG concepts understandable for PYMES owners who may not have sustainability expertise. Avoid technical jargon without explanation.
4. **Bilingual-ready** — Default to Spanish for Latin American audiences. Use English only for international-facing content.

### Writing Rules

- Always spell "Sustenttia" with double 't'. Never write "Sustentia", "Sustentía", or "Sustentia".
- In Spanish content, use the acronym ASG (Ambiental, Social, Gobernanza). In English content, use ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance).
- Position sustainability as a competitive advantage: cost savings, market access, investor readiness, risk reduction.
- Avoid guilt-based or fear-based messaging. Focus on opportunity.
- Use concrete examples and data points when possible — PYMES owners respond to practical evidence.
- When referencing Sustenttia's diagnostic tools, describe outcomes (what the business learns) not just features (what the tool does).

## Content Rules

### Co-branding with CleantechHUB

- When co-branding is appropriate, add "Powered by CleantechHUB" in a smaller font in the footer area.
- Use Sustenttia's color palette as primary; CleantechHUB branding is secondary.
- Never blend the two color palettes — keep them visually distinct with clear separation.

### Social Media

- Hook with a business problem that PYMES face (rising costs, supply chain risk, market access).
- Present Sustenttia's ASG approach as the solution.
- CTA should drive to consultation booking or diagnostic tool.
- Hashtags: #Sustenttia #Sostenibilidad #PYMES #ASG + topic-specific.

### Documents and Reports

- Header: Sustenttia logo (left) + document title (right).
- Footer: "© [Year] Sustenttia" + page number.
- Use the blue-teal-gold palette for charts, graphs, and data visualizations.
- Structure reports with executive summary first — PYMES owners are time-constrained.

## Quality Checklist

Before delivering any Sustenttia-branded content, verify:

1. "Sustenttia" is spelled with double 't' everywhere.
2. Colors are from the Sustenttia palette (blue/teal/gold), not CleantechHUB palette.
3. Typography uses Poppins/Lato stack.
4. Sustainability is framed as business advantage, not compliance.
5. ASG (not ESG) is used in Spanish content.
6. Content is accessible to PYMES owners without sustainability expertise.
7. Co-branding with CleantechHUB follows the hierarchy rules if present.
