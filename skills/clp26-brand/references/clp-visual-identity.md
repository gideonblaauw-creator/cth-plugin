## Color Palette

| Name | Observed Role | HEX Approx |
|---|---|---|
| **Deep Forest Green** | Primary background — all dark template variants | `#1A4A32` |
| **Lime / Yellow-Green** | Headlines, logo wordmark, CTA buttons, highlight text | `#C8E05A` |
| **Sky Blue** | Illustration element (sky in organic design motifs) | `#B8E4F0` |
| **Soft Green** | Rolling hills / earth in illustrations, accent panels | `#6DB56D` |

| **White** | Body text on dark backgrounds, subheadings | `#FFFFFF` |

**Never use:** CleantechHUB blues as primary on CLP-branded posts. Keep brand layers separate.

## Typography

| Role | Style |
|---|---|
| **Programme label** (e.g. "CLIMATELAUNCHPAD") | Monospaced / condensed, white, small-caps treatment |
| **Milestone headline** (e.g. "Call for Start-ups") | Bold sans-serif, white, large |
| **CTA text** (e.g. "Apply now") | Extra-bold sans-serif, Lime, very large |
| **Supporting info** (deadline, location) | Monospaced or condensed sans-serif, white, medium |
| **Body / story text** | Bold + regular sans-serif, white (bold for key phrases, regular for supporting) |
| **Quote text** | Extra-bold sans-serif, white, maximum size on card |

Font family is a geometric/humanist sans-serif (similar to Inter, Neue Haas Grotesk, or Aktiv Grotesk).

## Design Motifs & Illustration Style

The CLP visual language uses an **organic, nature-inspired illustration** system:

- **Curved leaf/petal shape** — large, sweeping organic arc in deep green, creates a "window" into the sky/landscape
- **Sky & landscape** — light blue sky + rolling green hills + warm ochre path, viewed through the organic opening
- **Thin line decoration** — delicate lime/yellow crossing lines in corners (geometric accent)
- **Atmosphere:** Optimistic, forward-looking, the "opening" motif suggests opportunity and growth

This illustration always occupies the **right half** of landscape-orientation posts or the **upper-right** corner of square posts.

**Text placement:** Always on the LEFT or LOWER-LEFT, on solid dark green — never over the illustration.

---

## Layout Principles

1. **Dark green solid block** on the left/bottom = text zone
2. **Organic nature illustration** on the right/top = visual zone
3. **Lime yellow** for the single most important text element (CTA or headline)
4. **White** for all supporting text
5. **Logo bar** at the bottom: ClimateLaunchpad (left) + Organiser Logo (right, in white box)
6. **Programme label** at top: monospace small caps header above the main headline

### Hierarchy in a typical M2 post:
```
[TOP]    [INSERT PROGRAMME NAME] CLIMATELAUNCHPAD  ← small, white, monospace
         Call for Start-ups                         ← medium, white, bold

[CENTER] Apply now                                  ← LARGE, lime, extra-bold
         DEADLINE: DAY, MONTH                       ← medium, white, monospace

[BOTTOM] Climate Launchpad logo    Organiser Logo box
```

## What NOT to Do

- ❌ Do not place lime text on white backgrounds in CLP templates
- ❌ Do not mix CleantechHUB blue palette into CLP-green templates
- ❌ Do not use the organiser logo outside its designated bottom-right box
- ❌ Do not remove the "Powered by Climate KIC" attribution
- ❌ Do not use fonts that aren't the geometric sans-serif of the system
- ❌ Do not place text over the organic illustration area
- ❌ Do not modify the logo sizes or proportions
