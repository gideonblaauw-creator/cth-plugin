*Source: https://www.pvblic.org/rein-hubs*

## What is REIN?

**REIN** = Resilience Entrepreneurship Innovation & Nature

REIN is a global platform that transforms environmental challenges into investment-ready business opportunities by supporting entrepreneurs through hubs embedded in national development strategies across the Global South.

**Led by:** Pvblic Foundation and CleantechHUB
**Backed by:** Global family offices, multilateral partners, and the US Department of State (origin programme)

**Origin story:** REIN evolved from a successful US State Department pilot (CCE Hubs, 2021–2024) into a permanent, multilateral platform. Pvblic Foundation deployed and managed the regional hubs. In 2025+, REIN expanded into a permanent programme.

---

## Why REIN Matters for CLP26

REIN's 5 hubs are the **physical and operational reason CLP26 exists in these 5 countries**. They provide:
- Local entrepreneurship infrastructure (workspace, curriculum, technical support)
- An existing pipeline of 1,000+ sustainable ventures across the region
- Relationships with universities, governments, and the private sector
- Credibility as the regional delivery partner for ClimateLaunchpad

When CLP26 launches in Colombia, Peru, El Salvador, Guatemala, and Costa Rica — it launches *through* REIN's hub network.

## REIN + CleantechHUB Relationship

CleantechHUB is explicitly named as co-leader of REIN alongside Pvblic Foundation. In CLP26 communications:
- CleantechHUB = the organiser (runs the programme, owns the brand layer)
- Pvblic Foundation/REIN = the supporter and hub infrastructure (enables delivery in each country)
- Both share credit — never position one against the other

**Correct framing:** *"CleantechHUB, en alianza con REIN Hubs de Pvblic Foundation..."*
**Avoid:** Any implication that they are separate or competing organisations in this context.
