# CLP26 Brand & Campaign Skill

This skill ensures all CleantechHUB communications for the ClimateLaunchpad 2026 campaign
are visually consistent, tonally correct, and strategically effective for driving applications
from Latin America.

## The Three Brand Layers

Every CLP26 piece operates within **three nested identities**. Always apply all three — they do not conflict but each has a defined role:

### Layer 1 — ClimateLaunchpad Global Brand (primary visual identity)
The official CLP templates from Climate-KIC define the visual look and feel for all milestone posts.
→ See `references/clp-visual-identity.md` for full details.

### Layer 2 — CleantechHUB Programme Brand (organiser identity)
CleantechHUB is the local programme organiser. Its logo appears as the "ORGANISER LOGO" in all CLP templates.
→ See `/mnt/skills/user/cleantechhub-brand/SKILL.md` for full details.

### Layer 3 — REIN Hubs / Pvblic Foundation (supporter & infrastructure identity)
REIN (Resilience Entrepreneurship Innovation & Nature) is the regional hub network operated by Pvblic Foundation and CleantechHUB. REIN's 5 hubs in Colombia, Peru, Costa Rica, Guatemala, and El Salvador are the **physical and operational infrastructure** through which CLP26 is delivered in Latin America. Pvblic Foundation is the lead backer of REIN.
→ See `references/rein-pvblic-identity.md` for full details.

**Layer hierarchy for content decisions:**
- **Visuals:** Layer 1 (CLP templates) always sets the frame
- **Organiser logo / local branding:** Layer 2 (CleantechHUB) in the organiser slot
- **Supporter attribution:** Layer 3 (REIN / Pvblic Foundation) in captions, footers, and "con el apoyo de" credits
- **Copy tone:** Layer 2 (CleantechHUB) governs language, with REIN's mission narrative adding depth for longer-form content

## Supporter Attribution (MANDATORY in every post)

All three supporters MUST be credited in every post — either in the visual or in the caption:

| Supporter | Role | How to credit |
|---|---|---|
| **REIN Hubs / Pvblic Foundation** | Regional hub infrastructure & lead supporter | *"con el apoyo de REIN Hubs by @PvblicFoundation"* or tag @pvblic |
| **Climate-KIC** | Global programme power | Shown in all CLP visuals as *"Powered by: Climate KIC"*; tag @ClimateKIC in captions |
| **CleantechHUB** | Local programme organiser | Organiser logo in visual; mention as *"organizado por @CleantechHUB"* when needed |

**Standard attribution line for captions (short form):**
> *Con el apoyo de REIN Hubs by @PvblicFoundation y @ClimateKIC 🌍*

**Standard attribution line for captions (long form, e.g. LinkedIn):**
> *Organizado por @CleantechHUB · Con el apoyo de REIN Hubs by @PvblicFoundation y @ClimateKIC 🌍*

**Context note:** REIN Hubs are *the reason* CLP26 exists in these 5 countries. Pvblic Foundation built and operates the hub network across Colombia, Peru, El Salvador, Guatemala, and Costa Rica. When writing longer-form copy, this backstory can be surfaced to add credibility:
> *"Gracias a la red de REIN Hubs de Pvblic Foundation, ClimateLaunchpad llega por primera vez a [país]."*

## Campaign Structure: 5 Milestones

The campaign follows the official CLP milestone framework. Each milestone has designated visual templates.

| Milestone | Content Type | Available Templates |
|---|---|---|
| **M1 — Partnership Announcement** | "X has joined CLP26" | `Milestone_01a/b` — dark green + lime, organic leaf motif |
| **M2 — Call for Applications** | "Apply Now" / "Convocatoria Abierta" | `Milestone_02a/b/c` — dark green + sky/nature illustration |
| **M3 — Startup Announcements** | Selected startups reveal + success stories | `Milestone_03a/b` — grid of logos OR story format |
| **M4 — Events / Workshops** | Bootcamp, pitching events, webinars | `Event_template_01a/b/c` — with date/location/CTA |
| **M5 — Post-Programme Results** | Impact numbers, gender stats | `Milestone_05` — icon + stats infographic |

**Quote templates** (`Quote_01a/b`) can be used at any milestone to amplify founder or mentor voices.

## Audience Targeting

**Primary audience:** Innovators, entrepreneurs, students, and purpose-driven people in Colombia, Peru, El Salvador, Guatemala, and Costa Rica who want to create positive climate impact.

**Psychographic profile:**
- Believe their idea can change the world
- Motivated by purpose, not just profit
- May feel their idea is "not ready" — our tone must break that barrier
- Respond to social proof (alumni, stats, other founders like them)
- Inspired by community and belonging

**Audience segments to address across the campaign:**
1. **Young innovators / students** — "Tu idea importa aunque sea un boceto"
2. **Women entrepreneurs** — "¡Mujeres, llegó la hora!" (direct address worked in past campaigns)
3. **Purpose-driven professionals** — pivoting to climate impact
4. **Existing founders** — already have a cleantech idea, need the push to apply

## Tone of Voice

**Overall campaign tone:** Positive, energising, "can-do". Never doom-and-gloom. We believe people
can solve climate change — and CLP is their launchpad.

| Dimension | Guidance |
|---|---|
| **Energy** | High energy, exclamation marks welcome (¡…!), urgent but joyful |
| **Empowerment** | Speak directly to the audience — TÚ, VOSOTROS, ¡TÚ PUEDES! |
| **Inclusivity** | Reach across gender, background, experience level |
| **Credibility** | Back claims with programme scale ("el mayor concurso de ideas verdes del mundo") |
| **Action bias** | Every post ends with a CTA (Apply, Share, Save the date, Tag a friend) |

**Key Spanish-language phrases to use:**
- *"¡Sé el cambio que quieres ver en el mundo!"*
- *"¡Por un futuro sostenible!"*
- *"La competencia de ideas verdes más grande de Latinoamérica y el Caribe"*
- *"De la IDEA a la ACCIÓN"*
- *"¡Aplica ya!"* / *"Convocatoria abierta"*
- *"Tu idea puede cambiar el mundo"*
- *"¡Este programa es para ti!"*

**Phrases to AVOID:**
- Climate doom / catastrophism ("el planeta está muriendo")
- Over-technical jargon without explanation
- Elitist framing ("solo para expertos / solo para startups avanzadas")
- Passive voice — always active and direct

## Social Media Platform Guidelines

### LinkedIn
- Longer-form captions (150–300 words)
- Professional framing: programme credentials, global reach, Climate-KIC partnership
- Tag: @ClimateLaunchpad @ClimateKIC @PvblicFoundation
- Include application link
- Best for: M1 partner announcements, M3 startup spotlights, M5 results

### Instagram
- Short punchy caption (50–100 words) + emoji rhythm
- Lead with the hook in line 1 (before "more" cutoff)
- 5–8 hashtags in first comment or end of caption
- Best for: M2 call-for-applications, quote cards, event promos, reels

### Facebook
- Mid-length (80–150 words), conversational
- Include the full application link prominently
- Works well with carousels (multiple template variants)
- Best for: community targeting in each country

### Newsletter
- Subject line: curiosity + urgency (e.g. "¿Tienes una idea que puede salvar el planeta?")
- Opening: personalised address to the reader
- CTA button: "APLICA AHORA →" linking to https://climatelaunchpad.org/application-form/
- Include supporter logos (Pvblic + Climate-KIC)
