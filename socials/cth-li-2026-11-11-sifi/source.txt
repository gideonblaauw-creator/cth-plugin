🌱 Catalysing investment in resilient agriculture, forests and land use

Investing in sustainable land use is essential to addressing climate change, protecting biodiversity and strengthening livelihoods. Yet many promising solutions in developing countries still struggle to attract the investment they need.

As a founding partner of the SDG Impact Finance Initiative (SIFI), SECO is pleased to announce a new Call for Proposals under SIFI’s Investment Window.

The initiative invites commercially viable impact investment funds contributing to more sustainable and climate-resilient agriculture, forestry and land use in developing countries.

Selected funds can receive up to CHF 10 million in junior equity to help de-risk investments and mobilise additional private capital.
The call focuses on solutions in areas such as:
 
🌳Agroforestry and forest management
🌱Land restoration and climate-smart agriculture,
🦋Biodiversity conservation

A particular emphasis is placed on gender equality.

📅 Key deadlines:
Clarification requests: 16 November 2026
Stage 1 applications: 30 November 2026
