How these numbers were collected

  • Own-site only. Figures come from each startup's website or its own published materials — not third-party databases or estimates.
  • Verbatim quotes. Wording is copied as published; spelling and units stay as on the page.
  • No inference. If a site gives no tCO₂e figure, the startup is omitted from this deck.
  • Missing periods. Empty period cells mean "not stated on source page".

Metric types (do not add across types).

  • mitigated — emissions reduced or absorbed as described by the company.
  • avoided — counterfactual savings vs. a baseline the company describes.
  • managed — greenhouse gases handled through the firm's programs.

Headline counters show separate totals per metric type. Never sum mitigated + avoided + managed into one portfolio headline.

Data file: research/nexus-co2/co2.csv · Country, sector, and theme from Nexus cards when absent in CSV.
