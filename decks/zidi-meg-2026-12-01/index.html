Participant Q5 & Q6

Political risk diligence — and what diaspora capital does differently

Political / regulatory diligence (Q5) — flip cards

Sector rules & permits

MubOn: charging licences in Bogotá and Cali mapped in six multi-stakeholder workshops (public page).

Tariffs & price regulation

What can be charged, indexed, or re-set by government — diligence question for any grid-tied asset.

Election & policy cycle

Which EV and charging incentives survive a handover — stress-test before a diaspora ticket.

FX & capital controls

How dividends or repayments cross borders — core diaspora concern for Colombia/Ecuador tickets.

Contracts & legal structure

MubOn: enforceability, minority protections, fiscal host trail for P4G/WRI grant G3470 (public narrative).
