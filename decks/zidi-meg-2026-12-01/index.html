Zidi thesis checklist · applied to MubOn

Here is the readiness test I use before I take a climate SME to investors

  • Market trend: national EV growth and charging interoperability policy (PNIC target 3,000+ new stations by end 2026).
  • Sustainability of the company: kWh revenue, cost-share met, grant catalytic not permanent.
  • Growth metrics: sites, kW, sessions, users, revenue reconciled in one public narrative.
  • Externalities: emissions avoided, jobs, gender mix reported; negative externalities not itemised on the public page.
  • Mid- and long-term ambition: reach investment-ready scale within the grant window (no disclosed target beyond that).

Participant Q5 & Q6

Political risk diligence — and what diaspora capital does differently

My regulatory checklist (Q5) — click to tick

  • Sector rules & permits: licences that bind the asset — MubOn mapped charging rules in Bogotá and Cali.
  • Tariffs & price regulation: what can be charged, indexed, or re-set by government.
  • Election & policy cycle: which incentives survive a handover.
  • FX & capital controls: how dividends or repayments cross borders.
  • Contracts & legal structure: enforceability, minority protections, and fiscal host if grants were involved.
