Four Stages from Idea to Scale

Stage 1

Activation

2–3 weeks · Ideation

Idea validation, team formation, bootcamp, lean canvas, initial pitch.

Stage 2

Incubation

10 weeks · Building

Product–market fit, MVP development, weekly mentoring, workbooks, pitch deck, business-model canvas.

Stage 3

Acceleration

8–12 weeks · Scaling

Business-model refinement, investor connections, financial modeling, growth strategy.

Stage 4

Cross-Continent Growth

Ongoing · Offtake + Capital

Market expansion, LatAm ↔ West Africa pilot corridors, European corporate offtake, follow-on funding.
