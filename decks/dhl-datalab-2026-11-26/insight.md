# Datalab insight: Air4U × DHL through a client layer

**source: DHL Group 2025 public report (may differ from the PDF Miguel shared)**

## Verdict: **partial** (strong for last-mile air quality; weak until Colombia scope is confirmed)

The client layer is **specific** where the Group report is road-heavy in both climate and air pollution: logistics GHG is **23% road** (p.26), while Scope 1 **NOx** and **PM10** in 2025 are dominated by air in the Group inventory (p.45)—yet **road transport still accounts for 6,131 t NOx and 344 t PM10** at Group level, the same fleet class DHL Colombia last-mile and PUD electrification targets address (46.2% e-vehicles today, **66% by 2030**, p.24). Air4U can make that road slice **locally measurable** in Bogotá, which the deck does not do.

The layer is **only partial** because every cited KPI is **Group-wide**. Colombia is not in the emissions narrative (only on the **myShares** country list, p.66). Without DHL Colombia depot boundaries, parcel volumes, and Bogotá fleet mix, pilots risk staying illustrative. GHG **intensity** is also absent (checked full deck), so throughput normalization must be negotiated tomorrow.

Air pollution is **not ESRS-material** for DHL Group (p.45), which explains the thin disclosure—and is exactly why a Datalab sandbox adds value: **make the non-material topic material for a city operations team**.

## Three concrete pilot ideas (tied to reported targets)

1. **Bogotá PUD electrification evidence pack** — Track **street-level PM2.5/NOx** on fixed last-mile corridors before/after e-vehicle deployment, benchmarked against **RMCAB** city stations. Tie narrative to Group **66% e-vehicle PUD by 2030** (p.24) and **<29 m metric t CO2e logistics GHG by 2030** (p.23). *Success signal:* measurable corridor exposure delta linked to fleet share change (client-supplied).

2. **Depot air-quality halo** — Deploy Air4U sensors around **1–2 DHL Colombia facilities** to quantify **NOx/PM2.5** gradients versus RMCAB background, correlated with shift patterns. Anchor to Group **Scope 1 road NOx (6,131 t)** and **PM10 (344 t)** as the inventory class being localized (p.45), not as Bogotá attribution. *Success signal:* repeatable depot index for ESG storytelling and worker/community dialogue.

3. **Decarbonization effects dashboard slice** — Feed monthly **realized decarbonization effects** logic (Group **2.1 m metric t CO2e in 2025**, target **2.5 m by 2026**, p.10) with a Colombia **module** showing EV miles and corridor air improvements as qualitative contributors. *Success signal:* DHL Colombia leadership can see how local air data supports Group steering KPIs without inventing national totals.
