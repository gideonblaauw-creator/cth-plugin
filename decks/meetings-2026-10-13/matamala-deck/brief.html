4 · Partnership model sketch Proposals

Options to explore today — not agreed terms.

A · Chile data provider (Open Data → Datalab)

ODS Cero publishes audited programme indicators on its Open Data platform; CTH ingests selected datasets into Datalab with shared metadata (dimensions: buen vivir, ecological, economic, social). CTH provides hosting/visualization; ODS Cero retains ownership and community consent.

Ask: Open Data roadmap, APIs/formats, audit standards, and what may be shared internationally.

B · Local datathon & lab anchor

Co-branded event or cohort using #/datathon and #/lab with LAURAS / regional cooperatives (IV–VI). CTH supplies platform; ODS Cero supplies problems, mentors, and Chilean participants.

Ask: 2026 milestone calendar (Christian mentioned upcoming announcements) and university partners (FCA UChile).

C · Client & ecosystem referral

Member cooperatives (IspirareCoop, CIBV, etc.) or COMEX partners use #/client for reporting, ESG dashboards, or circular metrics. CTH offers Datalab as infrastructure; ODS Cero offers trust and local delivery.

Ask: Which member needs data tooling first; whether referral is nonprofit-to-nonprofit or fee-based.

D · Governance & MoU (lightweight)

Document roles: data controller (ODS Cero), platform operator (CTH), public licence for anonymized aggregates, bilingual comms, and exit clause. Defer commercial terms until pilot scope is defined.
