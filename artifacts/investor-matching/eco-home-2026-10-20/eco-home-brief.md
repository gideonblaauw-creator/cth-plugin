## 1. Public-source profile

### What it is

**My Ecohome** (also written Ecohome and Eco Home) is a Colombian social enterprise in home, personal-care, and garden products made from plant extracts. The live store sells degreaser, dish soap, multipurpose cleaner, fruit-and-vegetable wash, and laundry detergent, in bottles through 20 litre sizes, priced in Colombian pesos. [mundoecohome.com](https://www.mundoecohome.com/)

The company FAQ says the products are biodegradable, the packs are 100 percent recyclable, and the line covers personal care (liquid hand soap, insect repellent), home cleaning, and garden care. It names retail in **Éxito, Carulla, Jumbo, and Home Center**, among others, plus Instagram and Facebook as “Ecohome Colombia”. Larger sizes: `info@myecohome.co`. [FAQ](https://www.mundoecohome.com/pages/preguntas-frecuentes)

On 29 September 2026, Maritza López Parra wrote that the brand is in almost all of the large Colombian retailers, with a natural home-and-garden portfolio, and pointed to `mundoecohome.com` and Instagram `@ecohomecolombia`. [LinkedIn](https://es.linkedin.com/posts/maritza-l%C3%B3pez-parra-8a96ab1a_ecohome-productos-de-limpieza-para-el-hogar-activity-7510750332096970752-gTXJ)

The climate and social model, as Acumen describes it, is **productive restoration**. Rural families plant native *Sapindus saponaria* (chumbimbo). The fruit becomes bioproducts that replace petrochemical ingredients in home care and personal care. Planting is described as improving the local microclimate, attracting pollinators, regenerating soil, and reducing erosion, while families earn from the fruit. Acumen’s stated aim is ecological regeneration of **1 percent** of Colombia’s tropical dry forest and income for more than **700** rural families. [Acumen, 27 October 2025](https://acumen.org/blog/acumen-invests-ecohome-tropical-dry-forest-colombia/)

As of that October 2025 announcement, more than **220** families were in the Red de Protectores del Chumbimbo. The 2030 target is **700** partner families. Acumen cites the Humboldt Institute and the Ministry of Environment: the tropical dry forest has lost over **90 percent** of its original cover, and about **720,000 hectares** remain, highly fragmented. [Acumen](https://acumen.org/blog/acumen-invests-ecohome-tropical-dry-forest-colombia/)

El Tiempo says this investment expands the work in **southwestern Antioquia**, where Ecohome already runs the productive-restoration model. [El Tiempo, updated 20 October 2025](https://www.eltiempo.com/vida/medio-ambiente/inversion-para-restaurar-el-bosque-seco-tropical-y-fortalecer-comunidades-rurales-en-antioquia-3500387) A NaturaTech LAC note on an earlier special recognition places a related regeneration project in **Cartama province, Antioquia**, with Nativas.
