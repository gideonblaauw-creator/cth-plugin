# AI Security Manifesto (short)

**Version:** 1 (DRAFT) · **Date:** 14 Nov 2026 · **Status:** Pending Gideon approval

## 150-word summary

Security behind closed doors protects model owners, not the public. In an agentic, tokenized economy, AI moves money, signs contracts, and reports impact. Latin American finance must not run on systems it cannot inspect. I learned from climate and impact data at CleantechHUB that closed numbers invite greenwashing and open, verifiable data unlocks capital. Open models and open weights let auditors, regulators, and local institutions test behavior without single-vendor lock-in. My principles: openness by default, scrutiny over secrecy, data sovereignty, verifiable agents with human accountability, tokenized assets tied to auditable data, climate data as infrastructure, fair access, and humans who answer when agents fail. At Americas Innovation Council I am contributing to the first report on AI and cybersecurity in LatAm finance and planning a round table with The AI Frontier at Stanford. I ask builders, banks, regulators, and investors to reward what can be verified. Trust must be testable.

## One line

An open, agentic, tokenized world is secure because anyone can verify it.
